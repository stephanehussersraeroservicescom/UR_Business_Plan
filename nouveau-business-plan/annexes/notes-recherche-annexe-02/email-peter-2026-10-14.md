# Email to Peter (OneWorld), draft of 14/10/2026

Subject: Urban Rig technical annex: points to confirm (PVC and tyre tests, gas, chlorine, dioxins, mass balance, water treatment)

Dear Peter,

We are finalising the technical annex of the Urban Rig business plan, working from the original test reports and OneWorld documents. A few points need your confirmation so that the figures we show to investors and to the French permitting authorities are fully documented. For each question I give the document and page we used.

**Priority: PVC and tyre tests**

1. Tests on PVC and tyres are becoming urgent for us. Could you run, or share if they already exist, tests on (a) PVC-rich plastic waste and (b) end-of-life tyres, with laboratory analysis of the oil (chlorine, split between organic and inorganic; sulphur; calorific value), the char and the gas? For tyres, we have the demonstration report of 12 December 2020 on the 4.8 t machine (Urban-Rig-Demo-Operation-Test-Report.pdf). Any laboratory reports on those products would be very useful.

**Gas analysis**

2. KANSO Technos report B2002600 (sampled on 25 November 2020; Analysis-Results-Off-gas-Odor-Exhaust.pdf, p. 1): the sampling point is left blank, and the gas contains 65.8 % nitrogen and 12.3 % oxygen. Where was the sample taken (before or after the catalytic layer, after the burner, at the stack)? Was the line purged with nitrogen or diluted with air? Do you have an analysis of the undiluted gas, with its flow rate and calorific value?

**Chlorine in the oil**

3. Japan Lubricating Oil Society reports 20221020-003-03 and -04 of 31 October 2022 (Chiba report, PDF p. 45 and 46): the oils from the Noda packaging plastics and non-combustible residue contain 0.137 % and 0.526 % chlorine. Could you confirm that these oils come from the runs of 9 and 15 September 2022? The test method is not stated, and both oils show an acidic reaction. Is this total chlorine, and is it mainly organic (chlorinated hydrocarbons) or inorganic (dissolved hydrogen chloride or salts)? A measurement separating the two would help us a lot.

4. The Chiba report (printed p. 23, PDF p. 29) states that the gas from the pyrolysis furnace passes through a catalytic reaction tank before the condenser. Is this the same chlorine adsorption catalytic layer as on the URC-2000 (URC-2000 brochure, p. 4)? What type of catalyst was it, and was it new or already loaded during the September 2022 runs? Do you have chlorine measurements on oil produced by a URC-2000 with the catalytic stage in operation?

**Oil composition**

5. The GC-MS analysis of a "drain" sample by the Osaka Research Institute of Industrial Science and Technology (gaschromatography.pdf) does not identify the sample. Which machine, feed, date and sampling point does it come from? Has the formal report been issued? To assess the potential of the oil for jet fuel or SAF, a mass-based composition and a distillation curve of the oil would also be very useful.

**Stated mass balance (URC-2000 brochure, p. 5)**

6. For 200 t of household waste with 10 % resins, the brochure gives 25 % char, 5 % oil (10,000 L), 5 % off-gas (10,000 L) and 0 to 5 % metals and glass. Do the 5 % oil and 5 % off-gas come from the 10 % resin fraction? Where do the remaining 60 to 65 % go (water evaporated from food waste, for example)? Are the percentages by mass or by volume, since 200 t is also shown as 200 m³?

**Dioxins**

7. We have two results that are not directly comparable:
   - 0.067 ng-TEQ/m³: Oosumi provisional report dated 18 October 2022, Noda test unit, run of 15 September 2022 on non-combustible residue (Chiba report, printed p. 50);
   - 0.011 and 0.025 ng-TEQ/m³: Kankyo Kogai Bunseki Center, letter of 27 October 2023, "waste plastic liquefaction demonstration facility", 26 and 27 September 2023 (Dioxins .pdf, p. 1).

   Which machine and site were measured in 2023, and what changed between the two (machine version, flue gas treatment, feed)? Could you share the final reports of both campaigns, with the oxygen content of the sampled gas (we need to correct values to 11 % oxygen, the EU reference), and measurements of carbon monoxide and hydrogen chloride?

**Process water and SEA CORAL**

8. The Chiba report (printed p. 33) states that the test unit had no wastewater treatment, and the raw water exceeded the discharge standards (for example phenols 740 mg/L, cyanide 14 mg/L, pH 0.8 and BOD 16,000 mg/L). The SEA CORAL data we have is a seawater desalination test. Has SEA CORAL been tested on Urban Rig process water? We would need the removal of phenols and cyanide, the capacity per tonne of waste, the handling of the concentrate and the cost.

Any documents you already have would be welcome first, even if some tests take longer. I am happy to go through these points on a call if that is easier.

Stéphane
