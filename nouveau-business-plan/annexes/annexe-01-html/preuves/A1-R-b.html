Evidence 1.R.b

Directive 2010/75/UE, article 3 points 40 et 41 (installations d'incineration et de co-incineration) et article 42(1) (exclusion de la pyrolyse et de la gazeification sous conditions)

What this evidence shows

Industrial Emissions Directive: pyrolysis and gasification plants are waste incineration plants when their products are incinerated, unless the gases are purified and emit no more than natural gas.

Confidence

100 %

EU legislation

How it was obtained

The facts were read directly in the source document below, at the location indicated, and recorded in the project register under IED-2010-75-ART3-42.

Quotation (original language)

  • « This Chapter shall not apply to gasification or pyrolysis plants, if the gases resulting from this thermal treatment of waste are purified to such an extent that they are no longer a waste prior to their incineration »
  • « such as pyrolysis, gasification or plasma process » (art3_40)

Values and content read

As recorded in the project register (in French).

  • Une installation d'incineration de dechets comprend les autres procedes de traitement thermique, tels que la pyrolyse, la gazeification ou le plasma, si les substances qui en resultent sont ensuite incinerees. Le chapitre IV ne s'applique pas aux installations de gazeification ou de pyrolyse si les gaz sont purifies au point de ne plus etre des dechets avant leur incineration et ne peuvent pas emettre plus que la combustion du gaz naturel.
