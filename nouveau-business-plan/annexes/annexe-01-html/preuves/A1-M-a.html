Evidence 1.M.a

Brochure OneWorld anglaise (oct. 2019), mecanisme de recuperation de l'huile et procede continu

What this evidence shows

OneWorld's description of the process: superheated steam at 600 °C in an oxygen-free reactor, chlorine catalyst, condenser, separation of gas oil and heavy oil by solenoid valve, oil and water separation; a continuous plant as 7 or 4 batch-type units at fixed temperatures; temperature of each unit, 90 minutes per step, start-up of 8 batches. Also OneWorld's statements on mixed waste, no sorting and non-oxidised metals (p. 2).

Confidence

95 %

Manufacturer documentation (OneWorld), English brochure, October 2019

Quotation (original language)

  • « Inject high-temperature oxygen-free steam(further superheated) at 600 °C into the cracking furnace. ; The vaporized gas is treated with chlorine catalyst and liquefied by a cooling condenser. ; Segregate gas-oil and heavy-oil by the temperature difference of vaporized gas through a solenoid valve. ; Water is separated in an oil/water separation tank, remove impurities in a filtration tank, and recover recycled oil. »
  • « URBAN RIG makes inside the rector high temperature and oxygen free by superheated steam and recover the fuel oil from the resin-based mixed waste c/w food residue and mud. ; URBAN RIG does not require to sort waste for processing and able to vaporize and carbonize the waste by superheated super-fine particles steam. Furthermore, metals can be extracted in a non-oxidizing state. ; This machine does not require clean such contami- nation and able to input after one-day storage to remove moisture. » (p2)
  • « In the continuous process type, the single batch type structures are installed at 7 or 4 places with the specifications suitable for the purpose. » (p14_continu)
  • « The continuous unit process has a temperature setting for each unit. ; 7-continuous process, 90 minutes ; 3 bucket movement in 90 minutes ; 24 hours throughput: 17 units 51 buckets » (p15)

Values and content read

Version continue : 7 ou 4 unites de type lot en ligne, temperature fixe par unite.
