Evidence 1.R.a

Directive 2008/98/CE relative aux dechets (version consolidee du 05/07/2018), article 3 points 15 et 17, article 4(1)

What this evidence shows

Waste Framework Directive: definitions of recovery and recycling (recycling excludes reprocessing into fuels) and the waste hierarchy.

Confidence

100 %

EU legislation

Quotation (original language)

  • « It includes the reprocessing of organic material but does not include energy recovery and the reprocessing into materials that are to be used as fuels or for backfilling operations; »

Values and content read

As recorded in the project register (in French).

  • Valorisation (recovery) : operation dont le resultat principal est que des dechets servent a des fins utiles en remplacant d'autres matieres. Recyclage : valorisation par laquelle les dechets sont retraites en produits, matieres ou substances ; n'inclut pas la valorisation energetique ni le retraitement en matieres destinees a servir de combustible. Hierarchie : prevention, preparation en vue du reemploi, recyclage, autre valorisation (notamment energetique), elimination.
