OneWorld statements are admitted as a working basis when they appear in a OneWorld document cited with page or date; they are labelled CONSTRUCTEUR, attributed in the text and never presented as independently verified.

D-032. Annexe 3 : base du calcul du CO2 evite par rapport a l'incineration (charge riche en resines)

Working assumption for the CO2 avoided compared with incineration (resin-rich feed): the fossil carbon of the feed is emitted in both routes; the oil replaces heavy fuel oil at equal energy; the incinerator replaces energy as per ADEME; purchased electricity at the French average mix. Not counted: transport, flue gas treatment, char and surplus gas sold, process losses.

Dated 2026-10-15. D-074. Huile : chlore et point d'eclair traites comme deux sujets separes ; essais d'Urban Rig

Decision of the project sponsor (25 and 28 November 2026): chlorine and flash point of the oil are treated as two separate subjects. Chlorine: a series of Urban Rig tests, with as baseline the capture of the chlorine carried by the superheated steam and its droplets with calcium carbonate, the purified water being returned to the process; other methods will follow. Flash point: the separation valve is set to send the light fraction to the burner with the gas; the sponsor proposes a setting of 50 °C, and the cut temperature is to be fixed by tests.
