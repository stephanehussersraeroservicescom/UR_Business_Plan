# Annex 7. Industrial and Commercial Waste (France)

This annex describes the mixed non-hazardous waste of businesses in France (déchets d'activités économiques en mélange, DAE): how much there is, what it contains, and what an Urban Rig could produce from it. Plastic streams collected on their own, such as stretch film or production scrap, belong to Annex 8 [hyp:D-018]. Residual household waste is covered by Annex 5.

## How to Read This Annex

OneWorld, the manufacturer of the Urban Rig, is the sponsor of this business plan [hyp:D-027]. Its documentation is marked "OneWorld design data"; these are the company's own figures and have not been verified independently.

Each figure or fact is followed by a small link to its evidence sheet. Sheets numbered 7.x belong to this annex; sheets numbered 2.x, 3.x, 5.x and 8.x are those of Annexes 2, 3, 5 and 8. All sheets are listed in the index of evidence sheets.

**Data limits.** Business waste is far less measured than household waste. No national characterisation campaign exists for mixed business waste; the composition below comes from a visual survey of 18 sorting plants, which its authors describe as approximate [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. No moisture, calorific value or chlorine measurement was found (request R-048).

**Revision.** This version was revised after an independent check of the annex. The changes are listed, point by point, in the project's register of discrepancies for Annex 7.

## Key Messages

- **A large but poorly measured stream, ample for one machine.** Businesses produced 57 million tonnes of waste in France in 2021, excluding construction [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. Establishments of 10 or 20 employees or more produced about 3,289 kt of mixed non-hazardous waste in 2022 [calc:CALC-A7-MELANGE-TOT-KT]. Ile-de-France produced 5.09 Mt of non-hazardous, non-inert business waste in 2020, excluding waste handled by the public service [A7-PRPGD-IDF-SUIVI3-2023], of which at least 2.77 Mt was mixed [A7-PRPGD-IDF-SUIVI3-2023]. One URC-2000, about 51,100 t a year [calc:CALC-A8-CAPACITE-AN] [hyp:D-035], would take about 1.0 % of the first figure and 1.8 % of the second [calc:CALC-A7-PART-MACHINE-DAE-IDF-PCT] [calc:CALC-A7-PART-MACHINE-MELANGE-IDF-PCT]. Volume is not the constraint; a stable and characterised quality is.
- **Mixed business waste is poorly recovered.** Only 34 % of it is recovered, and 32 % is incinerated without energy recovery or landfilled [A7-INSEE-IP2034-2025].
- **It is a poor oil feed.** Mixed flows at sorting plants hold only 5 % plastics, against 28 % wood, 19 % inerts and 34 % fines or unidentifiable material [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. One tonne would give 27.5, 36.5 or 40.5 kg of oil (pessimistic, standard, optimistic) [calc:CALC-A7-HUILE-BAS-KG-T] [calc:CALC-A7-HUILE-CENT-KG-T] [calc:CALC-A7-HUILE-HAUT-KG-T] [hyp:D-049].
- **Char would be the main product in mass**: 95 to 139 kg per tonne, mainly from wood [calc:CALC-A7-CHAR-BAS-KG-T] [calc:CALC-A7-CHAR-HAUT-V2-KG-T] [hyp:D-049] [hyp:D-054]. About 21 % of the stream is class B wood [calc:CALC-A7-BOISB-PCT]. This wood contains chemical substances above natural levels [A7-FCBA-CLASSIF-BOIS-2022]; the quality and outlet of the char are not shown without analysis.
- **Its holders pay to get rid of it.** Sorting plants pay about 200 EUR per tonne to treat their rejects, tax included [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. This is the cost of today's outlets, not an entry price secured by the Urban Rig.
- **The plastic of businesses is better taken separately.** Ile-de-France businesses produce 260,000 t of plastic waste a year, half of the commerce share being stretch film (Annex 8) [A8-PRO-IDF-LIVRET-2023]. Its availability under contract and its price are not documented.

The national year is 2021 in section 1.1 of the ADEME report and 2020 in its summary [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]; this annex keeps 2021.

The mixed tonnage is calculated from the published shares, which are rounded to the unit: manufacturing 1,050 kt, commerce 1,165 kt, services 1,073 kt [calc:CALC-A7-MELANGE-IND-KT] [calc:CALC-A7-MELANGE-COM-KT] [calc:CALC-A7-MELANGE-SERV-KT]. The rounding of the shares makes the total an order of magnitude, not a precise figure. It excludes construction and small establishments; the source gives no mixed tonnage for them, so their effect on the total is not quantified [A7-INSEE-IP2034-2025].

**Ile-de-France.** The regional series is 5.90, 5.95, 5.63 and 5.09 Mt in 2014, 2016, 2018 and 2020; 2020 was affected by the lockdowns [A7-PRPGD-IDF-SUIVI3-2023]. Within it, mixed business waste was 3.41, 3.93, 3.35 and 2.77 Mt; the regional plan calls this total a minimum, as the mixed waste exported out of the region is not fully known [A7-PRPGD-IDF-SUIVI3-2023]. In 2020, 58 % of it entered a sorting plant, and the plan aims at 100 % in 2025 [A7-PRPGD-IDF-SUIVI3-2023].

Of the 2,228,079 t landfilled in the region in 2022, 38.1 % was delivered directly by businesses [A8-CST-ORDIF-ISDND-2023], about 848,898 t [calc:CALC-A7-IDF-DAE-ENFOUIS-T]. When the rejects of treatment plants are reallocated to their original producers, businesses account for 77.6 % [A8-CST-ORDIF-ISDND-2023], about 1,728,989 t [calc:CALC-A7-IDF-DAE-ENFOUIS-REAFF-T]; this second reading is the better measure of landfilled waste of business origin. Businesses also made up 8.8 % of the 3,810,761 t incinerated [A8-CST-ORDIF-UIDND-2023], about 335,347 t [calc:CALC-A7-IDF-DAE-INCIN-T].

- **Commerce and services** produce most of the mixed waste: a quarter of their ordinary waste [A7-INSEE-IP2034-2025].
- **The food industry** is 30 % of manufacturing waste, and 71 % of its waste is organic [A7-INSEE-IP2034-2025]; it goes to composting or anaerobic digestion rather than to an Urban Rig.
- **Sorted streams** are recovered at 79 % [A7-INSEE-IP2034-2025]; they are not a feed for the Urban Rig, except sorted plastics (Annex 8).

| Material, mixed flows at sorting plants (2023) | Share of mass | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Wood (three quarters class B) | 28 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |
| Inerts | 19 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |
| Fines or unidentifiable material | 34 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |
| Paper and cardboard ("fibrous") | 7 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |
| Plastics | 5 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |
| Metals | 3 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |
| Textiles | 3 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |
| Plaster | 1 % | [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025] |

The composition was estimated visually on 18 sites, which received 52 % mixed flows and 48 % single-material flows [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. It is not a national campaign. The authors note that visual identification is approximate compared with a formal characterisation, that each site has its own inputs and that inputs change over time [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. These shares are therefore a trend across the sites, not a feed specification; they describe the mixed flows received by sorting plants, not their rejects, nor the stream an Urban Rig would contract. Moisture, calorific value and chlorine were not measured (request R-048).

**What the Urban Rig cannot convert.** Inerts, metals and plaster make 23 % of the mass [calc:CALC-A7-NONCONV-PCT]. The fines, 34 % [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025], are of unknown composition: they may hold water, combustible matter and inerts.

**Treated wood.** Three quarters of the wood is class B [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. That is about 21 % of the mixed stream [calc:CALC-A7-BOISB-PCT]. The French classification of wood waste describes the former class B as non-hazardous wood waste containing chemical substances above the levels naturally present in biomass; it notes that treated wood may contain halogenated organic compounds or heavy metals [A7-FCBA-CLASSIF-BOIS-2022]. Chlorine and metals may therefore reach the char; no measurement was found on this stream (request R-052).

**Reading.**
- **Less than half the oil of household waste.** The standard case gives 36.5 kg of oil per tonne [calc:CALC-A7-HUILE-CENT-KG-T], against 87 kg for residual household waste (Annex 5) [calc:CALC-A5-HUILE-TOT-CENT-KG-T]: mixed business waste holds 5 % plastics [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025], household waste 15.79 % [calc:CALC-A5-CAT07-PCT].
- **The tonne is closed only on paper.** About 205 kg per tonne are not allocated in the standard case [calc:CALC-A7-NONALLOUE-CENT-KG-T], mostly the gas, liquid and water of the wood, 196 kg [calc:CALC-A7-BOIS-RESTE-CENT-KG-T]. With the fines, this mass has no measured yield. In the model, by default, it gives no heat and no saleable product [hyp:D-055]. The Urban Rig test must measure a full mass balance (R-021).
- **A char feed, if the wood is accepted.** Wood dominates the convertible part. Wood pyrolysis gave 26.2 to 27.6 % char in the literature (secondary source) [A5-CZAJCZYNSKA-2017-REVIEW]; the optimistic 40 % is above that range [hyp:D-045]. With about 21 % of the stream in class B wood [calc:CALC-A7-BOISB-PCT], the quality and outlet of the char cannot be assumed before chlorine and metals are measured (R-052). The standard case is the reference for a first estimate; the optimistic case is an upper bound, not a design value [hyp:D-054].
- **The energy balance cannot be built** without moisture and calorific value (request R-048). The plastic gas, 5.0 kg per tonne [calc:CALC-A7-GAZ-PLAST-KG-T], gives about 187 MJ per tonne [calc:CALC-A7-GAZ-PLAST-MJ-T], about 12 % of the burner demand of 1,507 MJ per tonne stated by OneWorld [calc:CALC-A2-BRULEUR-MJ-T] [calc:CALC-A7-GAZ-PLAST-PART-BRULEUR-PCT]. The energy of the gas from wood, fibrous material and fines is unknown; no self-sufficiency can be claimed.

These values are benchmarks of today's costs and editable fields of the model. The 200 EUR/t is what sorting plants pay to treat their rejects [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]; it becomes an Urban Rig entry price only through a contract, after transport, pre-sorting and the competing outlets (incineration, solid recovered fuel, landfill). No default entry price has been decided for mixed business waste; it is to be confirmed by a letter of intent or a contract (R-044). The default of the paid streams in Annex 8 is 100 EUR/t [hyp:D-042].

## 7.6 Operational Points

- **Variability.** Composition varies from one container to the next; the 18 sites ranged from mostly mixed flows (mechanised sites, 68 %) to mostly single-material flows (33 %) [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025]. A supply contract needs an admission specification, a sampling plan and refusal criteria (R-052).
- **Contaminants.** Treated wood, plaster (sulphur), inerts and metals require pre-sorting (Annex 9). Inerts, metals and plaster alone are 230 kg per tonne to extract and dispose of [calc:CALC-A7-NONCONV-KG-T].
- **Competition.** Sorting plants are required to divert mixed waste from landfill: the regional target is 100 % sorted in 2025 [A7-PRPGD-IDF-SUIVI3-2023]. Their rejects, which still cost about 200 EUR/t to treat [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025], may be a more accessible feed than raw mixed waste. Their composition is not that of section 7.3: the assumptions of this annex do not apply to them without a characterisation (R-052) [hyp:D-049].

## 7.9 Recommendations for the Business Plan

- Do not present mixed business waste as an oil feed: 36.5 kg of oil per tonne in the standard case [calc:CALC-A7-HUILE-CENT-KG-T].
- Consider the rejects of business waste sorting plants, which cost about 200 EUR/t to treat today [A7-ADEME-TRI-DAE-2025], and sorted business plastics (Annex 8); treat the 200 EUR/t as an avoided cost to negotiate, not as a secured price.
- Obtain a characterisation of a target stream (moisture, calorific value, ash, chlorine, sulphur, metals, fines, class of the wood) and an Urban Rig test with a full mass balance before any commitment (R-052, R-021).
- Use the standard scenario for a first estimate; keep the optimistic scenario as an upper bound [hyp:D-054].
