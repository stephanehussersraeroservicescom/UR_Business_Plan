Quotation (original language)

  • « Thermogravimetric analysis revealed primary devolatilization between 230–550 °C | Waste paper and textile wastes exhibit relatively lower pyrolysis oil yields (20–25 wt%) and higher char (28–32 wt%). | The gas yield for these materials remains comparatively high (45–48 wt%) | The pure plastics exhibit a markedly different trend, yielding the highest pyrolysis oil content (40–45 wt%) and the lowest char content (5–7 wt%). | Mixed plastics yielded the highest pyrolysis oil content (43 wt%) and the lowest char yield (16 wt%), whereas wastepaper produced the highest char (30–40 wt%). »

Values and content read

  • Resume : devolatilisation principale entre 230 et 550 C (analyse thermogravimetrique). Four rotatif semi-pilote, 2 kg par essai, 500 C. Papier et textiles : huile 20 a 25 % en masse, char 28 a 32 %, gaz 45 a 48 %. Plastiques melanges (LDPE et PP, moins de 2 % de PS et HDPE) : huile 40 a 45 %, char 5 a 7 % (corps du texte) ; le resume donne 43 % d'huile et 16 % de char. Le resume donne 30 a 40 % de char pour le papier. Eau de l'huile (tableau 3) : papier 8,01 % ; textile 3,21 % ; plastiques 1,22 %.
