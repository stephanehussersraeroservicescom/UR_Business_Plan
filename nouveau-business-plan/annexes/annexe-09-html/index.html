Annex 9. Equipment Configuration and Sorting Requirements

This annex describes the Urban Rig equipment, its price, what it needs around it (pre-treatment, utilities, building) and how the configuration changes with the feed: plastic waste (Annex 8), residual household waste (Annex 5), mixed business waste (Annex 7). The process itself is described in Annex 1.

How to Read This Annex

OneWorld, the manufacturer of the Urban Rig, is the sponsor of this business planD-027. Its documentation is marked "OneWorld design data"; these are the company's own figures and have not been verified independently. The price of the machine is set by the sponsorD-050.

Each figure or fact is followed by a small link to its evidence sheet. Sheets numbered 9.x belong to this annex; sheets numbered 1.x, 2.x, 3.x, 5.x and 8.x are those of other annexes. All sheets are listed in the index of evidence sheets.

Prices of auxiliary equipment. No quotation has been obtained for the pre-treatment line (request R-006). The prices shown come from public tenders, advertisements and manufacturer documents; they give orders of magnitude only.

Revision. This version was revised after an independent check of the annex. The changes are listed, point by point, in the project's register of discrepancies for Annex 9.

Key Messages

  • One price for the machine. The URC-2000, 200 t per day, costs 50 MEUR, including the options PRIMO (exhaust gas treatment), SEA CORAL (water treatment) and FORCE 9 (residue stabilisation); delivery is not included, as it depends on the siteD-050.
  • That is 978 EUR per tonne of annual capacity, on a reactor feed of 140 t per day over 365 days9.C.aD-035.
  • Installation included, shipping not. OneWorld design data state 100 forty-foot containers, 30 days of installation by 10 people and 30 days of training by 9 trainers1.M.h. Installation is included in the price; shipping varies widely with the site and is not countedD-051.
  • Around the machine: 10 MEUR. The sponsor takes about 5 MEUR per pre-treatment line and a base of 5 MEUR for the land, the building, civil works, engineering and permitsD-051.
  • 60 MEUR is the capital cost base, not the total financing need9.C.b. Shipping, grid connection and utilities not supplied with the machine, contingency and working capital come on top; the scope of each supplier is to be set in a contract matrix (request R-049). Contingency and working capital are handled in the financial model.
  • Pre-treatment depends on the feed. Sorted plastic needs little; residual household waste needs bag opening, screening and metal removal. OneWorld says no sorting is required, but its own plant layouts show a crushing hopper and a sorting machine1.M.b (request R-023).
  • These two amounts are estimates of the sponsor, not quotationsD-051; quotations remain to be obtained (R-006).

9.4 Options Included in the Price

  • PRIMO, exhaust gas treatment. Denitrification, desulphurisation, dust removal and deodorisation, presented by OneWorld as an option1.M.c. Its performance is discussed in Annex 2.
  • SEA CORAL, water treatment. A ceramic purifier that absorbs waste water, evaporates it with blown air and recovers clean water2.3.i. Its only documented test is the desalination of sea water2.3.j. Its performance on the process water of the Urban Rig is not documented (request R-017).
  • FORCE 9, residue stabilisation. Catalytic stabilisation of residues at about 400 °C, according to OneWorld1.M.c.

All three are included in the price of 50 MEURD-050.

9.7 Building and Site

  • The layout of the URC-2000 shows a building of 4,650 m21.M.b and a foundation load of 5 t/m21.M.c.
  • Earthworks, roads and civil works of a French waste reception site were estimated at 2,695,000 EUR excluding VAT in a 2024 tender9.L.f; the project is different and the figure is only an order of magnitude.
  • No price per square metre of industrial building has been sourced for this plan. The business plan takes a base of 5 MEUR for the land, the building, civil works, engineering and permits togetherD-051. With earthworks and civil works alone near 2.7 MEUR on a different French site9.L.f, this base is tight.

9.8 Engineering and Permitting

The plant needs an environmental permit (ICPE), studied in Annex 4. Textbook ratios for process plants put engineering and supervision at 4 to 20 % of fixed capital, and buildings at 2 to 18 % (secondary source)9.L.g. They are given for orientation only.

The pre-treatment amount applies to each line whatever the feed, in the reading of this annex; a plastic feed may need less (Section 9.6). Each amount is a parameter that the operator of the model can change.

What 60 MEUR does not cover. It is the base of the sponsor before shipping9.C.b. It does not show, as separate lines: grid connection and utilities not supplied with the machine, storage and fire protection, any additional flue gas or water treatment needed for the French permit, construction insurance, commissioning beyond the installation included, contingency and working capital. Some of these may already be in the machine price or in the 10 MEUR around itD-051; a contract matrix of supply limits is needed to avoid both gaps and double counting (request R-049). Contingency and working capital are set in the financial model.

9.11 Recommendations for the Business Plan

  • Use 60 MEUR per line before shipping as the capital cost base: the machine at 50 MEUR, installation included, plus 5 MEUR of pre-treatment and 5 MEUR of land, building, civil works, engineering and permits9.C.bD-050D-051. Add contingency and working capital in the financial model; do not present 60 MEUR as the total financing need.
  • Use at least 10 months for the machine in the timetable, plus French permitting (Annex 4).
  • Show shipping as a site-dependent line, and replace the two estimates by quotations when available (R-006, R-049).
  • Settle with OneWorld the electricity figure and the scope of pre-treatment (R-015, R-023).
