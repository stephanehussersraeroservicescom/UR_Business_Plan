Annex 7. Industrial and Commercial Waste (France)

This annex describes the mixed non-hazardous waste of businesses in France (déchets d'activités économiques en mélange, DAE): how much there is, what it contains, and what an Urban Rig could produce from it. Plastic streams collected on their own, such as stretch film or production scrap, belong to Annex 8D-018. Residual household waste is covered by Annex 5.

How to Read This Annex

OneWorld, the manufacturer of the Urban Rig, is the sponsor of this business planD-027. Its documentation is marked "OneWorld design data"; these are the company's own figures and have not been verified independently.

Each figure or fact is followed by a small link to its evidence sheet. Sheets numbered 7.x belong to this annex; sheets numbered 2.x, 3.x, 5.x and 8.x are those of Annexes 2, 3, 5 and 8. All sheets are listed in the index of evidence sheets.

Data limits. Business waste is far less measured than household waste. No national characterisation campaign exists for mixed business waste; the composition below comes from a visual survey of 18 sorting plants7.L.a. No moisture or calorific value was found (request R-048).

Key Messages

  • A large but poorly measured stream. Businesses produced 57 million tonnes of waste in France in 2021, excluding construction7.L.a. Establishments of 10 or 20 employees or more produced about 3,289 kt of mixed non-hazardous waste in 20227.C.a. Ile-de-France produced 5.09 Mt of non-hazardous, non-inert business waste in 2020, excluding waste handled by the public service7.P.a.
  • Mixed business waste is poorly recovered. Only 34 % of it is recovered, and 32 % is incinerated without energy recovery or landfilled7.P.b.
  • It is a poor oil feed. Mixed flows at sorting plants hold only 5 % plastics, against 28 % wood, 19 % inerts and 34 % fines or unidentifiable material7.L.a. One tonne would give 27.5, 36.5 or 40.5 kg of oil (pessimistic, standard, optimistic)7.C.b7.C.c7.C.dD-049.
  • Char would be the main product: 95 to 152 kg per tonne, mainly from wood7.C.e7.C.fD-049.
  • Its holders pay to get rid of it. Sorting plants pay about 200 EUR per tonne of rejects, tax included7.L.a.
  • The plastic of businesses is better taken separately. Ile-de-France businesses produce 260,000 t of plastic waste a year, half of the commerce share being stretch film (Annex 8)8.L.ak.

What the Urban Rig cannot convert. Inerts, metals and plaster make 23 % of the mass7.C.l. The fines, 34 %7.L.a, are of unknown composition.

Treated wood. Three quarters of the wood is class B, painted, varnished or glued7.L.a. Its coatings and glues may bring chlorine and metals into the char; no measurement was found.

Reading.

  • Less than half the oil of household waste. The standard case gives 36.5 kg of oil per tonne7.C.c, against 87 kg for residual household waste (Annex 5)5.C.j: mixed business waste holds 5 % plastics7.L.a, household waste 15.79 %5.C.e.

  • The energy balance cannot be built without moisture and calorific value (request R-048). The plastic gas, 5.0 kg per tonne7.C.n, is far below the burner demand of 1,507 MJ per tonne stated by OneWorld2.5.d.1.

7.6 Operational Points

  • Variability. Composition varies from one container to the next; the 18 sites ranged from mostly mixed flows (mechanised sites, 68 %) to mostly single-material flows (33 %)7.L.a.
  • Contaminants. Treated wood, plaster (sulphur), inerts and metals require pre-sorting (Annex 9).
  • Competition. Sorting plants are required to divert mixed waste from landfill: the regional target is 100 % sorted in 20257.P.a. Their rejects, which still cost about 200 EUR/t7.L.a, may be a more accessible feed than raw mixed waste.

7.9 Recommendations for the Business Plan

  • Do not present mixed business waste as an oil feed: 36.5 kg of oil per tonne in the standard case7.C.c.
  • Prefer the rejects of business waste sorting plants, which are paid about 200 EUR/t7.L.a, and sorted business plastics (Annex 8).
  • Obtain a characterisation (moisture, calorific value, chlorine) of a target stream before any commitment (R-048).
