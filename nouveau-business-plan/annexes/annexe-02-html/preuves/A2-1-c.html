Evidence 2.1.c

Rapport Chiba, bilans matiere et energie de quatre essais

What this evidence shows

Mass and energy balances of four runs (two PE/PP/PS mixed runs, two municipal waste runs): input, oil, char, metals, gas, electricity, gas oil, water, and energy ratios computed with fixed factors.

Confidence

100 %

Test report filed with the Chiba prefectural government

How it was obtained

Japanese original; the quotation below is reproduced in the original language. The figures were read directly in the source document below, at the location indicated, and recorded in the project register under CHIBA-BILANS.
