Evidence 2.2.a

Japan Lubricating Oil Society (潤滑油協会), analyses de deux huiles d'essai, rapports 20221020-003-03 et -04

What this evidence shows

Laboratory analyses of the two oils produced from Noda municipal waste: density, flash point, viscosity, pour point, carbon residue, water, ash, sulphur, nitrogen, chlorine and gross calorific value (calculated by the laboratory).

Confidence

100 %

Independent laboratory (Japan Lubricating Oil Society)

Japanese original; the quotation below is reproduced in the original language. The figures were read directly in the source document below, at the location indicated, and recorded in the project register under CHIBA-JLOS-HUILES.
