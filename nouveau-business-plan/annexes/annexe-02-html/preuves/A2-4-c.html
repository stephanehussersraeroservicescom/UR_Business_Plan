Evidence 2.4.c

Arrete du Gouvernement wallon du 27 fevrier 2003 (incineration et co-incineration des dechets), annexe VI : formule de correction a la teneur standard en oxygene

What this evidence shows

Formula to correct a measured concentration to the standard oxygen content.

Confidence

100 %

Regional legislation (Wallonia, transposition of EU incineration rules)

How it was obtained

The figures were read directly in the source document below, at the location indicated, and recorded in the project register under WALLONIE-2003-ANX6-O2.

Quotation (original language)

  • « ES = concentration d'emission calculee au pourcentage standard de la concentration d'oxygene »

Values read

As recorded in the project register (in French).

  • ES = (21 - OS) / (21 - OM) x EM ; ES concentration au pourcentage standard d'oxygene, EM concentration mesuree, OS oxygene standard, OM oxygene mesure.

Notes

  • Texte de plus de huit ans ; sert uniquement pour la formule de correction, identique dans son principe aux definitions de BAT-WI-2019-2010.
