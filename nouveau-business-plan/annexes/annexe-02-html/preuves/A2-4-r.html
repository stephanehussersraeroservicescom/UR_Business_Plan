Reglement (UE) 2023/2405 (ReFuelEU Aviation), article 3 et annexe I

ReFuelEU Aviation: definition of sustainable aviation fuels, including recycled carbon aviation fuels, and minimum shares from 2030.

Quotation (original language)

  • « 'sustainable aviation fuels' ('SAF') means aviation fuels that are either: synthetic aviation fuels; aviation biofuels; or recycled carbon aviation fuels »

Values read

As recorded in the project register (in French).

  • SAF = carburants d'aviation synthetiques, biocarburants d'aviation ou carburants d'aviation a carbone recycle. Carburants a carbone recycle = recycled carbon fuels au sens de la directive (UE) 2018/2001 art. 2 point 35, conformes au seuil de reduction d'emissions de l'art. 29a(2) et certifies selon l'art. 30. Parts minimales de SAF : 6 % en 2030, 20 % en 2035 (dont 5 % synthetiques), 70 % en 2050 (dont 35 % synthetiques).
