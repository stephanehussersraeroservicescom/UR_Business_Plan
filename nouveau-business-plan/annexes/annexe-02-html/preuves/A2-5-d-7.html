Evidence 2.5.d.7

Calculation · Energy balance of the continuous URC-2000

What this calculation gives

Energy in the gas divided by the stated fuel consumption, resin-rich feed (OneWorld design, 90 % resins).

Result: 2.163 rapport

Confidence

90 %

This calculation uses a working assumption decided by the project sponsor (sheet 2.5.0), so its confidence is capped at 90 %.

How it was calculated

The energy balance of the continuous URC-2000 uses OneWorld's figures, taken as correct by decision D-021 (sheet 2.5.0). Batch tests are used only for conversion coefficients.

  1. Heat demand. Burner rating 833 kcal/s, operated 24 hours a day, divided by 200 t/day (2.5.d.1).
  2. Stated fuel. 10,000 L/day of the plant's own oil, at the density and gross calorific value measured by JLOS, divided by 200 t/day (2.5.d.9). Used as the upper value of the fuel need.
  3. Energy in the products for three feeds:
  4. resin-rich feed (OneWorld design, English brochure p. 16);
  5. household waste with 10 % resins (OneWorld design, URC-2000 brochure p. 5);
  6. municipal packaging plastics (Noda test coefficients, per tonne of convertible material).

The values are 46,000 J/g for the oil, 37.4 MJ/kg for the gas (the sheets 2.5.c.x) and 32,090 J/g for the char. 4. Coverage. Energy in the gas divided by the heat demand and by the stated fuel.
