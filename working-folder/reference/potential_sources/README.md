## About Urban Rig Technology

Urban Rig provides cutting-edge waste recovery technology using continuous steam-cracking processes to convert mixed waste into sustainable oil, carbon, and recyclable metals. The system offers:

- **80%+ CO₂ savings** compared to conventional methods
- **No sorting required** - processes unsorted MSW
- **Minimal operator needs** - highly automated
- **Clean, safe operation** - closed-loop system
- **Globally deployable** - modular units adaptable to any region

## Global Applicability

**Typical Capacity**: 200 tons/day continuous processing (scalable: 50-500+ tons/day)
**Technology**: Oxygen-free steam cracking at 600°C
**Products**: Sustainable oil, carbon black, recovered metals
**Deployment**: Urban, peri-urban, or industrial locations worldwide

## Regional Adaptations

This business plan template can be customized for:
- **Europe**: Focus on circular economy compliance, REACH regulations, carbon credits
- **Asia**: High-volume urban waste, rapid growth, energy recovery priorities
- **Africa**: Decentralized solutions, off-grid energy, job creation
- **Americas**: Environmental justice, landfill diversion, renewable fuel standards
- **Middle East**: Industrial waste, petrochemical integration, water scarcity considerations

## Example Use Cases

**Urban Deployment** (e.g., Paris CDG Airport, Singapore, Dubai):
- Airport waste processing
- City municipal waste management
- Industrial park integration

**Regional Deployment** (e.g., West Africa, Southeast Asia):
- Multi-city waste management
- Agricultural waste processing
- Rural electrification support

**Industrial Integration** (e.g., refineries, chemical plants):
- On-site waste-to-feedstock conversion
- Circular economy closure
- Energy self-sufficiency
