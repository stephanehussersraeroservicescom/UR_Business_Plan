# 8. Implementation Timeline

## 8.1 Project Phases Overview

The Valorea project will be implemented in four main phases over approximately **24-30 months** from project initiation to full commercial operation.

```
Phase 1: Planning & Permitting (12-18 months)
Phase 2: Construction & Installation (6-9 months)
Phase 3: Commissioning & Pilot Operation (2-3 months)
Phase 4: Full Commercial Operation (Ongoing)
```

## 8.2 Phase 1: Planning and Permitting (Months 1-18)

### Month 1-3: Project Setup and Site Selection

**Key Activities**:
- Finalize joint venture agreements and legal structure
- Establish project team and governance
- Define technical specifications and requirements
- Conduct preliminary site surveys

**Deliverables**:
- JV company registration (Valorea)
- Project charter and governance documents
- Technical specification document
- Shortlist of potential sites (3-5 locations)

**Critical Path Items**:
- JV agreement negotiation
- Initial funding commitment

---

### Month 3-6: Site Acquisition and Engineering

**Key Activities**:
- **Site Selection**:
  - Detailed evaluation of shortlisted sites
  - Feasibility studies (technical, environmental, economic)
  - Negotiation and acquisition (purchase or lease)
- **Preliminary Engineering**:
  - Site layout design
  - Utility requirements and connections
  - Environmental baseline studies
  - Traffic and logistics planning

**Deliverables**:
- Site secured (contract signed)
- Preliminary engineering package
- Environmental baseline report
- Project budget refinement

**Critical Path Items**:
- Site acquisition (can be lengthy if negotiation or zoning issues)
- Environmental baseline studies (needed for permit application)

---

### Month 6-18: Regulatory Permitting

**Key Activities**:

**Environmental Permit (ICPE Registration)**:
- Prepare ICPE application dossier
- Submit to Prefecture
- Public inquiry period (1-2 months)
- Response to public comments
- Technical review by DREAL
- Authorization decree issued

**Timeline**: 12-18 months (typical for new industrial installations)

**Other Permits**:
- Construction permit (Permis de construire)
- Water use and discharge authorization
- Air emissions permit (integrated with ICPE)
- Waste transport and storage permits
- Occupational safety registration

**Deliverables**:
- ICPE authorization decree
- All necessary permits obtained
- Public engagement record

**Critical Path Items**:
- ICPE approval (longest lead time item)
- Addressing public concerns during inquiry period

**Risk Mitigation**:
- Engage early with regulatory authorities
- Comprehensive and transparent application
- Proactive community engagement
- Hire experienced permitting consultants

---

### Month 6-18: Detailed Engineering and Procurement (Parallel with Permitting)

**Key Activities**:
- **Detailed Engineering**:
  - Final site design and layout
  - Civil, structural, mechanical, electrical engineering
  - Process design and equipment specifications
  - Safety and environmental systems design
- **Equipment Procurement**:
  - Urban Rig unit manufacturing (long lead time)
  - Auxiliary equipment procurement
  - Control systems and instrumentation
  - Storage tanks and handling systems

**Deliverables**:
- Detailed engineering drawings and specifications
- Equipment orders placed
- Construction-ready package

**Critical Path Items**:
- Urban Rig unit manufacturing (6-9 months lead time)
- Major equipment procurement

---

### Month 12-18: Commercial Agreements

**Key Activities**:
- **Waste Supply Contracts**:
  - Negotiate with municipal authorities
  - Engage private waste collectors
  - Secure CDG Airport agreement
- **Product Offtake Agreements**:
  - Oil buyers (refinery, industrial fuel users)
  - Carbon black buyers (cement, agriculture)
  - Metal recyclers
- **Financial Close**:
  - Secure remaining project financing
  - Finalize loan agreements
  - Lock in equity contributions

**Deliverables**:
- Signed waste supply contracts (50%+ of capacity)
- Offtake agreements for major products
- Financial close achieved

**Critical Path Items**:
- Securing committed waste supply (needed for financing)
- Offtake agreements (reduce revenue risk)

## 8.3 Phase 2: Construction and Installation (Months 19-27)

### Month 19-21: Site Preparation and Civil Works

**Key Activities**:
- Site clearing and grading
- Foundation construction
- Underground utilities installation (water, sewer, electrical, gas)
- Road and parking construction
- Drainage and stormwater management
- Perimeter fencing and security

**Deliverables**:
- Site prepared and foundations ready
- Utilities connected
- Access roads completed

---

### Month 21-25: Equipment Installation

**Key Activities**:
- **Urban Rig Unit Delivery and Installation**:
  - Transport to site (oversize load)
  - Crane lifting and positioning
  - Foundation anchoring
- **Auxiliary Systems Installation**:
  - Feed conveyors and waste handling systems
  - Oil storage tanks and transfer systems
  - Carbon handling and storage
  - Water treatment and recycling system
  - Emissions control and monitoring equipment
- **Building Construction**:
  - Enclosure for equipment
  - Control room and office building
  - Maintenance workshop
  - Welfare facilities

**Deliverables**:
- All equipment installed and secured
- Buildings enclosed
- Mechanical completion

**Critical Path Items**:
- Urban Rig unit delivery and installation
- Integration of all systems

---

### Month 25-27: Electrical, Instrumentation, and Integration

**Key Activities**:
- Electrical wiring and connections
- Instrumentation and control system installation
- SCADA system setup and programming
- Safety systems testing
- Emissions monitoring equipment installation and calibration
- Integration testing of all systems

**Deliverables**:
- All systems electrically connected
- Control systems operational
- Pre-commissioning checks complete

## 8.4 Phase 3: Commissioning and Pilot Operation (Months 27-30)

### Month 27-28: Cold Commissioning

**Key Activities**:
- System-by-system functional testing (without waste)
- Leak testing and pressure testing
- Control logic verification
- Safety system testing
- Utility consumption testing
- Training of operations team (on-site with Urban Rig technicians)

**Deliverables**:
- All systems functionally tested
- Operations team trained and certified
- Punch list of minor items identified and resolved

---

### Month 28-29: Hot Commissioning

**Key Activities**:
- First waste introduction (small quantities)
- Start-up and heating of reactor
- Process parameter optimization
- Product quality testing
- Emissions monitoring and verification
- Fine-tuning of control systems

**Deliverables**:
- First oil, carbon, and metal production
- Process confirmed stable and safe
- Emissions within permitted limits

**Critical Path Items**:
- Achieving stable operation
- Product quality meeting specifications

---

### Month 29-30: Pilot Operation and Ramp-Up

**Key Activities**:
- Gradual increase in throughput (50% → 75% → 100% capacity)
- Extended operation testing (24/7 continuous operation)
- Performance guarantee testing (if applicable)
- Final regulatory inspections and approvals
- Handover from construction to operations team

**Deliverables**:
- Demonstrated capacity and performance
- Regulatory approvals for commercial operation
- Certificate of completion
- Formal handover to Valorea operations team

## 8.5 Phase 4: Full Commercial Operation (Month 30+)

### Month 30 Onward: Steady-State Operations

**Key Activities**:
- Continuous 200 tons/day processing
- Product delivery to offtake partners
- Ongoing optimization and improvement
- Regular maintenance and upkeep
- Regulatory compliance and reporting
- Community engagement and transparency

**Performance Targets** (Year 1):
- Uptime: >85% (learning curve)
- Throughput: 60,000-65,000 tons (due to ramp-up)

**Performance Targets** (Year 2+):
- Uptime: >90%
- Throughput: 70,000-73,000 tons/year

## 8.7 Critical Path Analysis

### Longest Lead Time Items

**1. ICPE Permitting: 12-18 months**
- Cannot start construction without approval
- Mitigation: Early engagement, comprehensive application, experienced consultants

**2. Urban Rig Unit Manufacturing: 6-9 months**
- Specialized equipment, limited production capacity
- Mitigation: Early order placement (during permitting phase)

**3. Site Acquisition: Variable (2-12 months)**
- Depends on availability and negotiation
- Mitigation: Start early, have backup options

**Total Critical Path Duration**: 24-30 months

### Opportunities to Accelerate

**Parallel Activities**:
- Engineering and procurement during permitting (at-risk approach)
- Commercial agreements during permitting
- Operator recruitment and training during construction

**Fast-Track Permitting**:
- Priority processing (if available for circular economy projects)
- Early consultation with regulators
- Complete and high-quality application

**Modular Construction**:
- Off-site fabrication of components
- Pre-assembled systems reduce on-site work

## 8.8 Risk Factors and Contingencies

### Schedule Risks

**Permitting Delays**:
- Risk: Public opposition, regulatory questions, incomplete application
- Contingency: +6 months buffer in timeline
- Mitigation: Proactive engagement, comprehensive application

**Equipment Delivery Delays**:
- Risk: Manufacturing issues, supply chain disruptions
- Contingency: +3 months buffer
- Mitigation: Contract penalties, alternative suppliers for auxiliary equipment

**Construction Delays**:
- Risk: Weather, labor availability, unforeseen site conditions
- Contingency: +2 months buffer
- Mitigation: Experienced contractors, flexible scheduling

### Recommended Overall Timeline

**Realistic Timeline**: 30 months (2.5 years)
**Conservative Timeline** (with contingencies): 36 months (3 years)
