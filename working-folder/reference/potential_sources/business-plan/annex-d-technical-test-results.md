## D.2 Oil Production Yields

### D.2.1 Validated Oil Yields by Feedstock Type

| Feedstock Type | Oil Yield (%) | Test Date | Laboratory |
|----------------|---------------|-----------|------------|
| **Pure PE (Polyethylene)** | 82% | Dec 2020 | Asada Shokai |
| **Pure PP (Polypropylene)** | 80% | Dec 2020 | Asada Shokai |
| **Pure PS (Polystyrene)** | 78% | Dec 2020 | Asada Shokai |
| **Clean plastic mix (PE/PP/PS)** | 72-82% | Dec 2020 | KANSO Technos |
| **Mixed plastic packaging** | 72% | Jan 2021 | KANSO Technos |

*Source: Urban Rig Demo Operation Test Report, Asada Shokai Certificate No. 20-12-142*

### D.2.2 Oil Yield Calculation Basis

At pyrolysis temperature of **600°C** in oxygen-free environment:
- PE/PP/PS plastics achieve **near-complete thermal decomposition** (95-100%)
- Oil recovery from decomposed plastics: **72-85%** of plastic mass
- Remaining mass converts to **syngas (10-15%)** and **carbon residue (5-15%)**

**Note on Mixed Waste**: When processing MSW with ~30% plastic content:
- **Oil yield from total input**: 21-26% (function of plastic percentage)
- **Oil yield from plastic fraction only**: 72-85% (unchanged)

*Source: Process engineering calculations based on validated laboratory data*
