Confidence scale

Each figure links to an evidence sheet that states a confidence rate (decision D-022, 14/10/2026).

RateApplies to
100 %External tests (test reports submitted to the authorities, independent laboratories); regulation and reference data.
95 %Manufacturer documentation; peer-reviewed literature; calculations on data rated 95 % or more.
90 %Calculations that use a working assumption; manufacturer tables with no printed author; patents.
80 %Secondary sources (the original document was not read, only a document that cites it).

A calculation is never rated above its least reliable input, and never above 95 %.

Labels

LabelMeaning
VÉRIFIÉVerified. Source read, then re-checked by a second reading (another agent or Stéphane).
LURead. Source document opened and read; not yet re-checked by a second reading.
CALCULÉCalculated. Formula shown, every input sourced. The result is never more reliable than its least reliable input.
HYPOTHÈSEAssumption. Declared working assumption, with who set it, when and why (decision reference D-xxx). It never becomes a fact by repetition.
CONSTRUCTEURManufacturer. Statement by OneWorld, taken from a OneWorld document cited with its page or date (decision D-016). Attributed to OneWorld in the text and never described as verified.

When nothing is found, the gap is shown as NON TROUVÉ (not found) and never filled with an estimate.
