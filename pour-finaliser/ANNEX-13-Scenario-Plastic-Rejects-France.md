# Annex 13 — Scenario Economics: Plastic Sorting Rejects (France)

**Purpose:** Full financial model for a URC-2000 deployment processing plastic sorting rejects (non-recyclable plastic packaging from French sorting centres). Combines the feedstock profile from Annex 8, pricing from Annex 3, equipment configuration from Annex 9, and country-level parameters from Annex 4A.

**Date:** March 2026
**Status:** PLACEHOLDER — to be completed when scenario modelling phase begins.

---

## Scope

This annex will contain:

1. **Feedstock parameters** — Near-100% plastic composition (Annex 8), 65--80% oil yield, minimal pre-processing
2. **Revenue projections** — Gate fees (€80--120/T, undercutting incinerator rates of €100--150/T), oil at highest yield, minimal biochar
3. **CAPEX** — Per Annex 9 §9.5.1 plastic rejects configuration (~€60.1M, lowest CAPEX due to minimal pre-sorting)
4. **OPEX** — Including external fuel cost (pure plastic feedstock requires purchased HFO — off-gas covers only ~39% of heating demand at 100% plastic)
5. **Energy balance** — Pure plastic scenario (Annex 2 §2.6.2, bottom row)
6. **P\&L, cash flow, and payback** — 15-year projection
7. **Sensitivity analysis**
8. **Plastic rejects--specific considerations** — Supply chain from sorting centres, CITEO relationship, chemical recycling classification under Loi AGEC, potential for highest-margin operation

**Reference:** The B1a financial model (`build_b1a_model.py`) contains a working version of this scenario.
