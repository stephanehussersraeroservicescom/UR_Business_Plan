## Data Reliability Notes

### High Confidence Data
- Global waste generation totals (World Bank, UNEP)
- OECD country statistics (Eurostat, national statistics)
- Regulatory frameworks (official government sources)

### Medium Confidence Data
- Developing country waste composition (limited measurement infrastructure)
- Informal sector contribution (by definition hard to measure)
- Projections beyond 2030 (subject to policy and technology changes)

### Estimated Data
- Open dumping percentages (many countries lack comprehensive data)
- Plastic waste fate in specific countries (limited tracking)
- Future technology adoption rates (Urban Rig deployment scenarios)

### Our Methodology for Estimates
Where official 2023 data is not yet published:
1. Use most recent official data (typically 2020-2022)
2. Apply documented growth rates from World Bank/UNEP
3. Note estimates clearly in text
4. Provide ranges rather than false precision
