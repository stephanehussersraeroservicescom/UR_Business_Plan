## 1. TARGET REFINERIES FOR URBAN RIG INTEGRATION

### Priority Target: TotalEnergies Gonfreville (Normandy)

**Location**: Gonfreville-l'Orcher, Normandy (150 km from CDG)

**Capacity**: 253,000 barrels/day

**Why This is the PRIME TARGET:**

**Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) Production**:
- Started SAF production in 2024
- Target: 160,000 tons SAF/year by 2025
- €70 million investment to process up to 100% circular economy waste
- **Signed 10-year agreement with Air France-KLM**: 1.5 million tons SAF through 2035

**Critical Need for Circular Economy Feedstocks**:
- SAF is made from "waste and residues from the circular economy"
- Co-processing these feedstocks in existing refinery units
- **Urban Rig pyrolysis oil = perfect circular economy feedstock**

**Direct CDG-Air France Connection**:
- Air France is based at CDG
- Air France needs SAF at CDG for all departing flights
- TotalEnergies Gonfreville supplies this SAF
- **SYNERGY**: Urban Rig at CDG processes waste → oil → Gonfreville → SAF → back to CDG for Air France

**Environmental Ambition**:
- CO₂ reduction: 75-90% over full lifecycle vs fossil fuels
- Part of TotalEnergies' net-zero by 2050 commitment
- Normandy platform has 14 decarbonization projects underway

**Current Technology Gap**:
- Relying on external waste/residue suppliers for feedstock
- No on-site advanced pyrolysis technology
- **Urban Rig fills this gap**: Direct supply of high-quality pyrolysis oil

**URL**: https://totalenergies.com/media/news/press-releases/totalenergies-begins-producing-sustainable-aviation-fuel-its-normandy

### Healthcare/Medical Waste (DASRI)

---

**📋 WHAT IS DASRI?**

**DASRI** = **D**échets d'**A**ctivités de **S**oins à **R**isques **I**nfectieux

**English**: Infectious Healthcare Waste

**Definition**: Medical waste that may contain microorganisms capable of causing infections, requiring specialized treatment before disposal.

**Examples**:
- Used syringes and needles
- IV bags and tubing
- Surgical instruments
- Blood-soaked materials
- Personal protective equipment (PPE) from infectious disease treatment
- Laboratory cultures and specimens

**Regulatory Status in France**: Highly regulated under French public health code; requires authorized collection, treatment, and disposal.

---

**DASRI Context**:
- Highly regulated in France under strict sanitary protocols
- Requires specialized treatment (typically incineration at 850°C+)

**Val d'Oise Medical Infrastructure**:
- Multiple hospitals and clinics in Val d'Oise department
- ABARIS: Specialized DASRI collection company operating in Val d'Oise since 1993
- Séché Healthcare, Ecodas: National DASRI processors

**DASRI Treatment Process**:
- Collection → Disinfection/sterilization → Incineration → Disposal
- **Current method: 100% incineration** (CO₂ intensive, no material recovery)

**Urban Rig Opportunity for DASRI**:
- **Pre-sterilized DASRI** (after autoclave treatment) could be processed by Urban Rig
- Convert post-treatment medical waste (mostly plastics: IV bags, syringes, PPE) into valuable oil
- **Challenges**:
  - Regulatory approval required (strict DASRI regulations)
  - Must demonstrate equivalent or better treatment than incineration
  - Potential concerns about residual contamination
- **Advantage**:
  - Material recovery vs. complete destruction
  - Lower CO₂ emissions vs. incineration
  - Hospitals seeking circular economy solutions for non-infectious medical waste

_**Estimated DASRI Volume (Val d'Oise region)**: 5,000-10,000 tons/year_

_**BASIS**: Order-of-magnitude estimate only. France generates ~170,000 tons/year DASRI nationally[CDG-4]. Val d'Oise (population ~1.25M) would represent ~2% of national total = ~3,400 tons/year. Range accounts for regional medical infrastructure concentration._

_**CRITICAL**: This estimate requires validation with actual data from:_
- _ABARIS (specialized DASRI collector in Val d'Oise)_
- _Séché Healthcare, Ecodas (national DASRI processors)_
- _Val d'Oise health authority (Agence Régionale de Santé - ARS)_
- _Individual hospital contracts and waste manifests_

_**This is a preliminary market sizing estimate only - DO NOT use for financial projections without validation.**_

**NOTE**: DASRI integration requires additional regulatory work and may be Phase 2 opportunity after proving MSW/commercial waste processing.
