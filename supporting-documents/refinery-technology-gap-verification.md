## CONFIRMED: Major French Refineries DO NOT Have On-Site Pyrolysis

### ✅ 1. TotalEnergies Gonfreville (Normandy) - **NO PYROLYSIS**

**Capacity**: 253,000 b/d
**Location**: Gonfreville-l'Orcher, Normandy

**What They Have**:
- Conventional refining and petrochemical operations
- NEW: SAF (Sustainable Aviation Fuel) production starting 2024
- Target: 160,000 tons SAF/year by 2025
- €70M investment for circular economy feedstock processing

**What They DON'T Have**:
- **NO on-site pyrolysis technology**
- **NO chemical recycling facility**
- **RELYING on external suppliers for circular feedstock**

**Critical Gap**:
- Gonfreville needs "waste and residues from the circular economy" as feedstock for SAF
- Currently buying this externally
- **Urban Rig would provide direct, local supply of pyrolysis oil for SAF production**

**Source**: TotalEnergies official announcements confirm SAF production but no mention of pyrolysis at Gonfreville

### ✅ 2. INEOS Lavera (Marseille) - **NO PYROLYSIS** (BUYING PYROLYSIS OIL)

**Capacity**: 210,000 b/d (refinery) + petrochemical cracker
**Location**: 30 miles west of Marseille

**What They Have**:
- Modified cracker to accept pyrolysis oil (June 2025)
- Virgin-quality recycled plastics production starting
- ISCC PLUS certification for circular polymers

**What They DON'T Have**:
- **NO on-site pyrolysis facility**
- **BUYING pyrolysis oil from external European suppliers**
- Oil is "made in Europe from post-consumer plastic packaging waste"

**Critical Quote**:
> "The INEOS cracker in Lavera has received the first deliveries of pyrolysis oil made from recycled plastic waste"

**Technology Gap**:
- Lavera is **actively purchasing** pyrolysis oil from external sources
- No information about who supplies it or volume available
- **Urban Rig would be a direct feedstock supplier** to their modified cracker

**Source**: INEOS press releases June 2025 confirm they're BUYING pyrolysis oil, not producing it on-site

### ❌ 6. TotalEnergies Grandpuits - **HAS PYROLYSIS** (Only One in France)

**Capacity**: Converting to "zero-crude platform" (no longer processing crude oil)
**Location**: 70 km southeast of Paris

**What They HAVE**:
- **Pyrolysis plant**: 15,000 tons/year capacity
- Partnership: TotalEnergies (60%) + Plastic Energy (40%)
- Technology: Plastic Energy's patented pyrolysis process
- Output: TACOIL (pyrolysis oil) for circular polymer feedstock
- **Status**: Commissioning mid-2025 (delayed from 2024)

**Why This is NOT Competition for Urban Rig**:
1. **Scale**: Only 15k tons/year vs Urban Rig's 70k+ tons/year per 200-ton unit
2. **Not operational yet**: Still commissioning as of mid-2025
3. **External technology**: Partnership with Plastic Energy, not in-house technology
4. **Proof-of-concept scale**: Small demonstration plant vs. commercial scale
5. **Zero-crude platform**: Site is being repurposed, not a typical refinery anymore
6. **Geographic**: 70km from Paris, not serving other refineries

**Potential Opportunity**:
- Grandpuits' small scale may prove demand for pyrolysis oil
- TotalEnergies may want to scale up with Urban Rig's continuous, larger-capacity technology
- Urban Rig could supply OTHER refineries (Gonfreville, Donges, Feyzin) that DON'T have pyrolysis

**Source**: Multiple TotalEnergies announcements about Grandpuits conversion and Plastic Energy partnership
