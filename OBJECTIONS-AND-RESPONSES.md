## CATEGORY A: FINANCIAL MODEL & VALIDATION (10 Items)

### A1. Gate Fee Assumptions Unverified (€80-150/ton)
**Severity:** 🔴 CRITICAL | **Status:** ⏳ PENDING

**The Objection:**
- All gate fees marked 🔴[VERIFY] but financial model depends on them
- French incineration: €50-90/ton (competitor pricing)
- Urban Rig target: €80-150/ton (10-67% premium over incineration)
- France has excess incineration capacity (38% vs 27.5% EU average) = downward price pressure

**Investor Concern:**
*"Why would municipalities pay €80-150/ton to Urban Rig when incineration costs €50-90/ton?"*

**Response Strategy:**
1. **TGAP Tax Advantage** (€15/ton saved vs incineration)
2. **Aircraft Waste Premium Pricing** (€120-200/ton for regulatory compliance)
3. **Differentiated Value Proposition** (95% recovery, 80-90% CO₂ reduction)

**Actions Required:**
- [ ] Contact Veolia, Suez, SEPUR for actual Île-de-France waste pricing
- [ ] Get ADP quote for current airport waste treatment costs
- [ ] Research SYCTOM incineration gate fees + TGAP
- **Deadline:** March 15, 2026

**Proposed Fix:** Create tiered pricing strategy:
- Tier 1 - MSW: €80-100/ton | Tier 2 - Industrial: €90-120/ton
- Tier 3 - Aircraft: €120-150/ton | Tier 4 - Composites: €100-150/ton

---

### A2. Oil Pricing Assumption (€500/ton) Not Confirmed
**Severity:** 🔴 CRITICAL | **Status:** ⏳ PENDING

**The Objection:**
- Revenue model depends on €500/ton oil sales (49% of total revenue!)
- No TotalEnergies Letter of Intent or price commitment
- Risk: TotalEnergies offers €300/ton instead of €500/ton

**Investor Concern:**
*"Revenue collapses by €4.6M/year if oil sells at €300/ton. How confident are you?"*

**Response Strategy:**
1. **SAF Feedstock Premium Justification** (EU mandates, double counting under RED II)
2. **Market Comparisons** (UCO €800-1,200, Tallow €600-900, Plastic pyrolysis €400-600)
3. **Diversified Offtake Strategy** (TotalEnergies primary, industrial boilers secondary, export tertiary)
4. **Floor price: €350/ton** if SAF market collapses

**Actions Required:**
- [ ] Secure TotalEnergies LOI with price range commitment
- [ ] Validate oil quality specifications (sulfur, chlorine, heating value)
- [ ] Identify 2-3 backup buyers
- **Deadline:** March 31, 2026

**Proposed Fix:** Build sensitivity (Base: €500/ton, Downside: €350/ton, Upside: €600/ton)

## CATEGORY B: MARKET & COMPETITIVE POSITIONING (6 Items)

### B1. Competing with Excess French Incineration Capacity
**Severity:** 🟡 HIGH | **Status:** ⏳ PENDING

**The Objection:**
- France incinerates 38% of MSW (excess capacity = downward price pressure)

**Response Strategy:**
1. **TGAP tax escalation** makes incineration more expensive over time
2. **Loi AGEC** discourages new incineration capacity
3. **Target niche waste streams** incineration doesn't want (aircraft, composites, EPS)
4. **ADP partnership** bypasses municipal competition

---

### B2. No Competitive Response Analysis (Veolia/Suez)
**Severity:** 🟡 HIGH | **Status:** ⏳ PENDING

**The Objection:**
- Veolia/Suez dominate French waste management. No analysis of their reaction.

**Response Strategy for 3 scenarios:**
- **Price war:** TGAP still applies to them; Urban Rig can match at €60-80/ton
- **Lobbying:** Urban Rig has superior emissions + ADP/TotalEnergies political support
- **Acquisition of competitor:** Urban Rig scale advantage (3-14× larger)
- **Alternative:** Partner with Veolia/Suez (collection → processing → products)

---

### B3. SAF Circular Loop Oversold (Only 4-5% of Air France Need)
**Severity:** 🟡 HIGH | **Status:** ⏳ PENDING

**The Objection:**
- Urban Rig = ~7-8k tons SAF/year. Air France needs 150k tons/year. That's 5%, not a "closed loop."

**Proposed Fix:**
- Remove "closes the loop" language
- Frame as "pilot demonstration" and "first of many"
- Show replication pathway: 10-20 facilities = 100% of Air France need

---

### B4. Dual-Unit Configuration Seems Financially Inefficient
**Severity:** 🟡 HIGH | **Status:** ⏳ PENDING

**The Objection:**
- 1×200T is 25% cheaper, but CDG chose 2×100T. Makes technology sound unproven.

**Proposed Fix:** Reframe rationale from "risk mitigation" to:
- **Feedstock optimization** (Unit 1: mixed MSW 30% oil, Unit 2: plastic-rich 40% oil)
- **Operational redundancy** (97% vs 93% uptime)
- **Phased CAPEX** (easier financing)
