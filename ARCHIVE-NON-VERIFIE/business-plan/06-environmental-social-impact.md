# 6. Environmental and Social Impact

## 6.1 Climate Impact: CO₂ Savings

### Quantified Emissions Reduction

Urban Rig delivers **80-90% CO₂ reduction** compared to conventional waste treatment methods.

#### Comparison per Ton of Waste Processed

| Treatment Method | GHG Emissions (kg CO₂e/ton) | Notes |
|------------------|----------------------------|-------|
| **Landfilling** | 400-600 | Methane release (CH₄ = 25x CO₂ impact) |
| **Incineration** | 300-500 | Direct CO₂ + fossil fuel auxiliary |
| **Urban Rig** | 50-100 | Mostly biogenic CO₂; minimal fossil input |
| **Net Savings** | **250-500 kg CO₂e/ton** | 80-90% reduction |

### Annual Impact for 200-Ton Facility

**Assumptions**:
- Processing capacity: 73,000 tons/year (200 tons/day × 365 days × 90% uptime)
- Average CO₂ savings: 350 kg CO₂e per ton

**Annual CO₂ Reduction**:
- **~25,000 tons CO₂e per year**

**Equivalent Impact**:
- **5,400 cars removed from roads** (assuming 4.6 tons CO₂/car/year)
- **3,100 hectares of forest** carbon sequestration (at ~8 tons CO₂/ha/year)

### Sources of CO₂ Reduction

**1. Eliminating Landfill Methane**:
- No anaerobic decomposition → no methane generation
- Methane (CH₄) is 25x more potent than CO₂ over 100 years

**2. Reducing Incineration Emissions**:
- No direct combustion of waste
- Lower energy input (self-powered by process gas)

**3. Fossil Fuel Displacement**:
- Recovered oil replaces virgin crude oil extraction and refining
- Biochar replaces coal in industrial applications
- Avoided upstream emissions from fossil extraction

**4. Biogenic Carbon**:
- CO₂ from biomass (food, paper) is carbon-neutral (part of short-term carbon cycle)
- Only plastic-derived CO₂ is fossil-based, but still lower than alternatives

## 6.2 Waste Diversion and Circular Economy Impact

### Diversion from Landfilling

**Current Situation** (Île-de-France):
- ~15-30% of MSW currently landfilled
- 200-ton Urban Rig facility diverts **73,000 tons/year** from potential landfilling

**Impact**:
- Reduces regional landfill dependency
- Extends lifespan of existing landfill capacity
- Supports EU goal: <10% landfilling by 2035

### Diversion from Incineration

**Current Situation**:
- ~40-59% of plastics and organics incinerated
- Urban Rig offers superior alternative

**Impact**:
- Reduces need for new incinerator capacity
- Lowers regional air pollution
- Recovers materials instead of destroying them

### Contribution to Circular Economy

**Material Recovery**:
- **Plastics** → Oil → Chemical feedstock for new plastics (chemical recycling loop)
- **Organics** → Biochar → Soil amendment (carbon sequestration, nutrient cycling)
- **Metals** → Non-oxidized recovery → High-quality recycling
- **Energy** → Self-powered process + surplus oil/gas

**Circular Economy Principles**:
1. **Keep materials in use**: Transform waste into valuable inputs
2. **Eliminate waste**: 95%+ material recovery rate
3. **Regenerate natural systems**: Biochar improves soil health and stores carbon

### Effective Recycling Rate Improvement

**Traditional System**:
- Recycling rate: ~42% (France, 2020)
- Remaining 58% → incineration or landfill

**With Urban Rig**:
- Recycling: 42% (unchanged - clean materials still recycled)
- Urban Rig recovery: +30-35% (residual streams)
- **Effective recovery rate: ~72-77%**

**Result**: Helps France meet 65% EU target and move toward 80%+ recovery.

## 6.3 Air, Water, and Soil Quality

### Air Quality Improvements

**Elimination of Landfill Emissions**:
- No methane (CH₄) releases
- No landfill gas flaring or fugitive emissions
- No odor pollution from decomposing waste

**Reduction in Incineration Emissions**:
Urban Rig facility processes waste that would otherwise be incinerated.

**Avoided incineration emissions**:
- Particulate matter (PM10, PM2.5)
- Nitrogen oxides (NOx)
- Sulfur oxides (SOx)
- Dioxins and furans
- Heavy metals volatilization

**Urban Rig Emissions** [VALIDATED: KANSO Technos Laboratory Report 20002610, December 2020]:

| Pollutant | Urban Rig Measured | EU Incineration Limit | Comparison |
|-----------|-------------------|----------------------|------------|
| **NOx** | 15 ppm | ~100 ppm | **85% lower** |
| **SOx** | < 1 ppm | ~20 ppm | **>95% lower** |
| **HCl** | < 1 ppm | ~7 ppm | **>85% lower** |
| **Dust/PM** | < 0.002 g/m³ | 0.01 g/m³ | **80% lower** |
| **H₂S** | < 0.5 ppm | N/A | Virtually zero |

**Odor Impact** [VALIDATED: KANSO Technos, November 2020]:
- Site boundary odor index: **< 10** (below detection threshold)
- No measurable odor impact on surrounding community

**Net Effect**: Urban Rig emissions are **5-20% of incineration limits** across all pollutants. This is laboratory-validated data, providing significant improvement in regional air quality.

---

### Water Quality Protection

**Elimination of Landfill Leachate**:
- Landfills generate toxic leachate (contaminated water)
- Risk of groundwater contamination
- Urban Rig: **zero leachate generation**

**Water Recycling**:
- Ceramic filtration system recycles process water
- Target: zero liquid discharge
- If discharge needed: meets or exceeds French standards
- Regular monitoring and compliance

**Minimal Water Consumption**:
- Closed-loop system reduces freshwater demand
- <1% of water use compared to traditional recycling (no washing/cleaning)

---

### Soil Protection

**Landfill Space Preservation**:
- 73,000 tons/year diverted = reduced land use
- Prevents soil contamination from landfill operations

**Biochar Soil Benefits**:
If carbon output used as biochar:
- **Improves soil structure**: Better water retention, aeration
- **Nutrient retention**: Prevents nutrient leaching
- **Microbial habitat**: Supports beneficial soil organisms
- **Carbon sequestration**: Stores carbon in soil for centuries
- **pH buffering**: Neutralizes acidic soils

**Agricultural Value**:
- Potential partnership with local agriculture
- Certified biochar production
- Closes urban-rural resource loop

## 6.4 Noise and Odor Control

### Noise Management

**Design Features**:
- Equipment enclosed in buildings
- Sound insulation on noisy components (pumps, conveyors)
- Buffer zones around facility

**Predicted Noise Levels**:
- **At property boundary**: <55 dB (residential), <70 dB (industrial)
- **Comparable to**: Light traffic, HVAC systems

**Mitigation**:
- Noise barriers if needed
- Restrict noisy outdoor activities to daytime
- Continuous monitoring and compliance

---

### Odor Control

**Waste Storage**:
- Enclosed bunker with negative pressure
- Air extracted and filtered through carbon filters
- Rapid processing minimizes storage time

**Process**:
- Sealed, oxygen-free system (no decomposition odors)
- Thermal destruction of volatile organic compounds
- Minimal odor generation

**Result**: Negligible odor impact beyond facility boundary.
