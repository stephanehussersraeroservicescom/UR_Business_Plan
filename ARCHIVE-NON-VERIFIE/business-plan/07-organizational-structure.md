# 7. Organizational Structure

## 7.1 Governance and Management

### Board of Directors

**Composition**:
- **2 representatives from Urban Rig Co. Ltd**
- **2 representatives from French partner entity**
- **1 independent director** (optional, for strategic guidance)

**Responsibilities**:
- Strategic direction and major decisions
- Budget approval and financial oversight
- Risk management and compliance
- Partnership agreements and major contracts

**Decision-Making**:
- Consensus-based for major decisions (both partners must agree)
- Simple majority for operational decisions
- Reserved matters: Technology changes, major capex, partner exit

### Executive Management

**General Manager (CEO)**
- Overall responsibility for Valorea operations
- Reports to Board of Directors
- Represents company to external stakeholders
- Background: Waste management or industrial operations experience

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Day-to-day operational management
- Financial performance and P&L
- Regulatory compliance
- Stakeholder relations (government, community, partners)
- Team leadership and development

---

**Technical Director**
- Urban Rig representative (can be seconded from Urban Rig Co. Ltd)
- Ensures technology performance and standards
- Liaison with Urban Rig global support

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Process optimization and troubleshooting
- Technical training and support for operations team
- Technology upgrades and improvements
- Quality control for outputs
- Interface with Urban Rig engineering team

---

**Operations Manager**
- Manages shift operations and production
- Ensures safety and environmental compliance
- Maintenance planning and execution

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Supervise shift operators (6-8 personnel)
- Production planning and scheduling
- Equipment performance monitoring
- Preventive maintenance program
- Incident investigation and corrective action

---

**Commercial Director**
- Manages waste intake contracts and product sales
- Develops new business opportunities
- Market intelligence and pricing strategy

**Key Responsibilities**:
- Negotiate tipping fee contracts with waste suppliers
- Secure offtake agreements for oil, carbon, metals
- Relationship management with buyers and suppliers
- Revenue optimization
- Contract compliance and invoicing

---

**HSE (Health, Safety, Environment) Manager**
- Ensures compliance with all French and EU regulations
- Manages environmental permits and reporting
- Workplace safety and training

**Key Responsibilities**:
- ICPE compliance and regulatory reporting
- Emissions monitoring and environmental management
- Safety training and drills
- Incident reporting and investigation
- Continuous improvement of HSE performance

### Shift Operations Team

**Shift Operators (6-8 personnel)**

**Structure**:
- 4 shifts rotating 24/7 coverage (e.g., 3 days on, 3 days off)
- 1-2 operators per shift depending on workload

**Qualifications**:
- Technical secondary education (Bac+2) or equivalent experience
- Industrial process experience preferred
- Willingness to work shifts
- Computer literacy (SCADA systems)

**Responsibilities**:
- Monitor automated control systems (SCADA)
- Conduct routine inspections and checks
- Manage waste intake during business hours
- Record production data and logs
- Respond to alarms and process deviations
- Basic troubleshooting and corrective actions
- Ensure HSE compliance

**Training**:
- Initial training: 2-4 weeks (Urban Rig + on-site)
- Ongoing training: Quarterly refreshers, annual recertification
- Cross-training on all positions for flexibility

---

### Maintenance Team

**Maintenance Technicians (1-2 personnel)**

**Structure**:
- Day shift coverage (Mon-Fri, with on-call rotation for emergencies)
- 1 senior technician + 1 junior/apprentice

**Qualifications**:
- Mechanical or electrical technician certification
- 3-5 years industrial maintenance experience
- Experience with thermal processing equipment preferred
- Troubleshooting and diagnostic skills

**Responsibilities**:
- Execute preventive maintenance schedule
- Troubleshoot equipment failures
- Coordinate repairs and spare parts
- Maintain equipment records and history
- Support commissioning and upgrades
- Interface with Urban Rig technical support

---

### Support Functions

**Logistics Coordinator (1 FTE)**
- Schedule waste deliveries
- Coordinate product pickups (oil, carbon, metals)
- Manage on-site storage and inventory
- Truck traffic management
- Documentation and traceability

**Administrative Support (0.5-1 FTE)**
- Invoicing and accounting support
- HR administration
- Document management
- Reception and general administration

## 7.4 Training and Development

### Initial Training Program

**Phase 1: Classroom Training (1 week)**
- Urban Rig technology overview
- Process chemistry and engineering
- Control systems and automation
- Safety procedures and emergency response
- Environmental compliance
- Quality control

**Phase 2: Hands-On Training (2-3 weeks)**
- On-site during commissioning
- Equipment familiarization
- Start-up and shutdown procedures
- Normal operations and monitoring
- Troubleshooting simulations
- Maintenance procedures

**Phase 3: Supervised Operations (2-4 weeks)**
- Gradual responsibility transfer
- Coaching by Urban Rig technicians
- Performance assessment and certification

**Total Initial Training**: 5-8 weeks per employee group

---

### Ongoing Training

**Annual Refresher Training**:
- Safety and emergency response
- Environmental compliance updates
- Process optimization techniques
- New equipment or procedures

**Specialized Training**:
- Advanced troubleshooting
- Maintenance techniques
- Quality management
- Leadership and management (for supervisors)

**External Training**:
- Industry conferences and workshops
- Regulatory compliance seminars
- Waste management and circular economy topics

**Budget**: €30,000-50,000/year

## 7.7 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### Operational KPIs

- **Throughput**: Tons processed per day/month/year
- **Uptime**: Availability % (target: >90%)
- **Yield**: Output products per ton of input
- **Energy Efficiency**: Self-sufficiency % and external energy use

### Financial KPIs

- **Revenue**: Total revenue from tipping fees + product sales
- **EBITDA**: Earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, amortization
- **Operating Margin**: EBITDA / Revenue
- **Cash Flow**: Operating cash flow and free cash flow

### Safety and Environmental KPIs

- **Safety**: Lost-time injury rate, total recordable incident rate
- **Environmental**: Emissions compliance %, waste diversion rate
- **Complaints**: Number and resolution time for community complaints

### Commercial KPIs

- **Waste Intake**: Volume by source, tipping fee average
- **Product Sales**: Volume and price for oil, carbon, metals
- **Customer Satisfaction**: Surveys and contract renewals
