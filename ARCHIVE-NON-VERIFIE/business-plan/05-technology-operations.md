# 5. Technology and Operations

## 5.1 Urban Rig Technology Deep Dive

### The Continuous Steam-Cracking Process

Urban Rig's patented technology uses **continuous thermal conversion** in an **oxygen-free environment** to break down complex organic and plastic molecules into simpler, valuable compounds.

#### Core Process Parameters

| Parameter | Specification | Purpose |
|-----------|--------------|---------|
| **Temperature** | 600°C | Optimal for molecular breakdown without combustion |
| **Atmosphere** | Oxygen-free (steam/inert gas) | Prevents oxidation; preserves metals; controls reactions |
| **Pressure** | Near-atmospheric | Safe operation; minimal equipment stress |
| **Residence Time** | Controlled continuous flow | Ensures complete conversion |
| **Catalytic Treatment** | Integrated | Enhances oil quality and gas composition |

### Process Flow Diagram

```
[Waste Input]
    ↓
[Pre-Screening] (remove oversized metals/inerts)
    ↓
[Continuous Feed System] (automated)
    ↓
[Thermal Reactor - 600°C, oxygen-free]
    |
    ├→ Vapor Phase → [Condensation] → LIQUID OIL
    |
    ├→ Gas Phase → [Separation] → SYNTHESIS GAS (recycled for heat)
    |
    └→ Solid Residue → [Separation] →
          ├→ CARBON BLACK / BIOCHAR
          └→ METALS (non-oxidized)

[Exhaust Gas Treatment] → Clean emissions
[Water Recycling System] → Minimal discharge
```

## 5.2 Continuous vs Batch Operation

### Why Continuous Processing is Superior

| Factor | Batch System | Continuous System (Urban Rig) |
|--------|--------------|-------------------------------|
| **Throughput** | Lower (downtime between batches) | Higher (24/7 operation) |
| **Labor** | High (manual loading/unloading) | Low (automated) |
| **Output Consistency** | Variable batch-to-batch | Consistent quality |
| **Energy Efficiency** | Heat loss during loading cycles | Stable thermal regime |
| **Operator Attention** | Constant monitoring | Automated controls |
| **Maintenance Complexity** | High (thermal cycling stress) | Lower (steady-state operation) |
| **Scalability** | Limited | Modular (add units) |

**Result**: Continuous operation provides **30-40% better economics** through higher utilization, lower labor, and consistent output.

## 5.3 Feedstock Logistics and Loading Systems

### Waste Acceptance Process

**Step 1: Incoming Inspection**
- Visual check for prohibited materials (batteries, pressurized containers, hazardous waste)
- Weighing for billing and tracking
- Documentation and traceability

**Step 2: Pre-Processing (Minimal)**
- **Magnetic separation**: Remove large ferrous objects
- **Shredding** (optional): Only if oversized items present
- **No sorting, washing, or drying required** - key advantage

**Step 3: Automated Feeding**
- Continuous feed conveyor system
- Controlled feed rate matching reactor capacity
- Buffer storage for consistent 24/7 operation

### Storage and Handling

**On-Site Storage**:
- Enclosed waste bunker (3-5 days capacity)
- Odor control (negative pressure, filtration)
- Fire suppression system

**Delivery Schedule**:
- Trucks arrive during business hours (8am-6pm)
- Unload into bunker
- Automated system feeds reactor 24/7

**Safety Measures**:
- No manual handling of waste in processing area
- Enclosed systems prevent exposure
- Fire detection and suppression

## 5.4 Output Handling Systems

### Liquid Oil Collection

**System**:
- Condensation of vapor phase
- Gravity separation of oil and water
- Storage tanks (temperature-controlled)

**Storage Capacity**:
- 50-100 tons on-site storage
- Allows flexible pickup scheduling
- Tank farm design meets French regulations

**Quality Control**:
- Regular sampling and testing
- Specifications agreed with buyers
- Traceability system

**Dispatch**:
- Tanker truck pickup (weekly or bi-weekly)
- Pipeline connection (if buyer adjacent) - future option
- ISO containers for export if needed

---

### Carbon Black / Biochar Handling

**System**:
- Continuous extraction from reactor bottom
- Cooling system
- Pneumatic or mechanical conveying to storage

**Storage**:
- Enclosed silos or big bags
- Dust control measures
- Dry storage (prevents degradation)

**Quality Grades**:
- Different applications require different specifications
- Potential for on-site screening/grading
- Moisture content control

**Dispatch**:
- Bulk trucks for large buyers (cement, fuel)
- Big bags for smaller customers (agriculture, activated carbon processors)

---

### Metal Recovery

**System**:
- Magnetic separation (ferrous)
- Eddy current separation (non-ferrous: aluminum, copper)
- Manual sorting for quality enhancement (optional)

**Storage**:
- Segregated by metal type
- Covered storage area
- Accumulated until economic lot size

**Dispatch**:
- Scrap metal dealer pickup (monthly or quarterly)
- Weight and quality verification

---

### Water Recycling

**Integrated Ceramic Filtration System**:
- Condensate from pyrolysis contains water vapor
- Ceramic filters remove particulates and impurities
- Recycled water returns to steam generation

**Discharge**:
- Target: **Zero liquid discharge** (closed-loop)
- If discharge required: meets French wastewater standards
- Regular monitoring and reporting

**Benefits**:
- Minimal freshwater consumption
- No wastewater treatment costs
- Reduced environmental footprint

## 5.5 Operator Requirements and Training

### Staffing Model

**24/7 Operation**:
- **4 shifts** rotating schedule (3 days on, 3 days off, or similar)
- **1-2 operators per shift**
- **Total operators needed**: 6-8 personnel

**Additional Roles**:
- **Maintenance technician** (day shift): 1-2 FTE
- **Logistics coordinator** (day shift): 1 FTE
- **HSE manager** (day shift): 1 FTE
- **Site manager** (overall responsibility): 1 FTE

**Total Team**: 10-13 personnel

### Operator Responsibilities

**Shift Operator Tasks**:
- Monitor automated control systems
- Conduct visual inspections
- Log data and production records
- Respond to alarms or deviations
- Coordinate waste intake (during business hours)
- Ensure HSE compliance

**Maintenance Technician Tasks**:
- Preventive maintenance schedule
- Repairs and troubleshooting
- Spare parts inventory
- Equipment performance monitoring
- Coordinate with Urban Rig technical support

### Training Program

**Initial Training** (provided by Urban Rig):
- Technology overview (theory and practice)
- Process control systems
- Safety procedures and emergency response
- Maintenance protocols
- Quality control and sampling

**Duration**: 2-4 weeks (combination of classroom and hands-on)

**Location**: Urban Rig training facility + on-site during commissioning

**Ongoing Training**:
- Annual refresher courses
- Updates on process improvements
- HSE training and drills
- Regulatory compliance updates

### Automation and Control

**Control System Features**:
- **SCADA (Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition)**: Real-time monitoring
- **Automated feed rate control**: Maintains optimal reactor conditions
- **Temperature and pressure monitoring**: Safety interlocks
- **Emissions monitoring**: Continuous compliance tracking
- **Remote diagnostics**: Urban Rig support can access system remotely

**Operator Interface**:
- User-friendly touchscreen HMI (Human-Machine Interface)
- Alarm management system
- Production reporting and analytics
- Mobile app for remote monitoring (optional)

**Result**: Minimal operator intervention required - system largely self-regulating.

## 5.7 Health, Safety, and Environmental (HSE) Protocols

### Safety Design Features

**Inherent Safety**:
- Oxygen-free atmosphere (no combustion risk)
- Operating temperature 600°C (not extreme high-temp)
- Near-atmospheric pressure (no explosion risk)
- Automated systems reduce manual handling

**Active Safety Systems**:
- Fire detection and suppression
- Emergency shutdown systems
- Gas detection (for any leaks)
- Pressure relief valves
- Ventilation and exhaust systems

### Environmental Controls

**Emissions Monitoring**:
- **Continuous emissions monitoring system (CEMS)** for stack gases
- Parameters: CO, CO₂, NOx, SOx, particulates, VOCs
- Real-time compliance tracking and reporting

**Emission Limits**:
- Will meet or exceed French ICPE requirements
- Target: <10% of incineration emission levels

**Odor Control**:
- Enclosed waste storage (negative pressure)
- Carbon filters on ventilation exhaust
- Minimal odor generation (waste quickly processed)

**Noise Control**:
- Equipment enclosed in buildings
- Noise barriers if needed
- Target: <55 dB at property boundary (residential), <70 dB (industrial)

### Workplace Safety

**Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)**:
- Standard industrial PPE for all personnel
- Respirators available for maintenance activities
- Fire-resistant clothing for reactor area work

**Safety Training**:
- Comprehensive initial training
- Monthly safety meetings
- Annual emergency response drills
- Hazard communication program

**Health Monitoring**:
- Pre-employment health screening
- Periodic health checks for operators
- Exposure monitoring (dust, heat, etc.)

### Emergency Response

**Emergency Scenarios and Protocols**:

**1. Fire**:
- Automatic suppression system activation
- Operator evacuation procedures
- Fire brigade notification

**2. Equipment Failure**:
- Automated shutdown procedures
- Isolation of affected systems
- Emergency repair protocols

**3. Feedstock Contamination** (hazardous material detected):
- Reject material before processing
- Isolate and safely store
- Contact appropriate disposal contractor

**4. Spill or Release**:
- Spill containment procedures
- Cleanup protocols
- Regulatory reporting (if required)

**Local Coordination**:
- Coordination with local fire department (pre-incident planning)
- Emergency contact list
- Annual review and updates
