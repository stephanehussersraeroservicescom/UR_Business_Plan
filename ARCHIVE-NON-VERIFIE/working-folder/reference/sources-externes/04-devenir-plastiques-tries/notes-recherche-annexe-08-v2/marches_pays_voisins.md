# Market proxies from France's neighbours: non-recycled / low-grade post-consumer plastic (residual waste, mixed fractions, sorting residues, chemical-recycling feedstock)

Research date: 2026-10-10. I used WebSearch and WebFetch only, and the web-search budget ran out before the end of the session. Quotes are reproduced as the WebFetch tool returned them. That tool sometimes summarises or translates, so check any quote against the source page before printing it in a business plan. Currencies are left as published (no conversion). "[calc]" marks my own arithmetic on cited figures.

**Reliability labels:** **mesuré** (sorting analysis or sampling with a stated method); **déclaré** (figures declared by an operator, a producer-responsibility organisation (PRO) or an authority); **contrat** (contract terms, tariff, tender or auction award); **indice** (trade-press price survey or index); **commentaire de presse** (press or expert commentary, or a teaser without data); **ancien** (data before 2022). The background folder `Refus plastiques pyrolyse vers combustible/` already holds disposal-cost tables (UK, DE, NL, BE-Fl, CH, ES and IT taxes and gate fees), the composition of sorting residues and the 2024 letsrecycle bottle and PTT series. They are reused here only where needed, and labelled "(background)".

## Q1. Germany: tonnages of LVP Sortierreste and Mischkunststoffe, where they go, and prices (DKR 350, Zuzahlung, EBS gate fees)

### Takeaway
Germany has solid **tonnage** data: about 0.69 Mt/yr of LVP Sortierreste (2020); 1.09 Mt of plastic packaging sent to energy recovery (2022); and, across all post-consumer plastic waste in 2023, 2.25 Mt burnt in MSW incinerators (MVA) plus 1.36 Mt used as EBS (Ersatzbrennstoff, refuse-derived fuel). Chemical recycling is still marginal at about 7 kt (2023). **No free source gives an absolute DKR 350 (Mischkunststoffe) price for 2022-2026.** EUWID publishes it behind a paywall, and the free bvse reports give only monthly changes or qualitative remarks. The usable German price anchors therefore sit on the disposal side: about 150 €/t paid by the waste holder to an EBS producer, 50 €/t paid by the EBS producer to the cement plant, a BEHG CO₂ surcharge of about 31 €/t on sorting residues in 2026, spot commercial waste at €80/t (late 2023), and RDF at −60 to +30 €/Mg (older source).

### Cited Findings
**Tonnages and destinations**
- Post-consumer plastic waste in Germany in 2023: "5,58 Mio. t" (p. 20). About 40.2 % went to MSW incinerators (MVA) and about 23.8 % to EBS in power plants and cement kilns (p. 20). Mechanical recycling took 1,952 kt (35.0 %, p. 21; 35.4 % in the text on p. 20). Chemical/feedstock use was 27 kt, which "enthält ~7 kt chemisches Recycling zur Gewinnung von Kunststoffrohstoffen" plus about 20 kt used as reducing agent (p. 19, fn. 3). Landfill was 27 kt, "Weniger als 1%". Export surplus was 0.28 Mt (p. 17, fn. 5). Household-near packaging totalled 1,967 kt and covers both LVP collection and residual waste (pp. 21-23). The report gives **no Sortierreste or Mischkunststoffe line**. — [Conversio, Stoffstrombild Kunststoffe 2023, Kurzfassung](https://vci.de/ergaenzende-downloads/kurzfassung-stoffstrombild-2023.pdf) — **déclaré** (survey plus modelling)
- The UBA data page (2023, based on Conversio) gives energy recovery of 3.61 Mt (61.1 %): "2.25 Mio. t" to waste incineration and "1.36 Mio. t" as Ersatzbrennstoff in cement works or power plants. Material plus chemical recycling was 2.27 Mt (38.4 %), and "Von Kunststoffabfällen aus privaten Haushalten wurden knapp 33 % stofflich verwertet". The page is internally inconsistent on the 0.5 % "rohstofflich/chemisch" figure. — [UBA, Kunststoffabfälle](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/daten/ressourcen-abfall/verwertung-entsorgung-ausgewaehlter-abfallarten/kunststoffabfaelle) — **déclaré**

In total, 2,120 kt was recycled ("stofflich verwertet", 65.9 %) and **1,090 kt went to energy recovery** (Zusammenfassung, p. 25). At the EU calculation point, plastics fall by 412 kt, i.e. −14.3 points (p. 29). The fetched text was the summary only, so Mischkunststoffe and Sortierreste tonnages were not visible. — [UBA Texte 156/2024](https://www.umweltbundesamt.de/sites/default/files/medien/11850/publikationen/156_2024_texte_verpackungsabfaelle_2022.pdf) — **déclaré**

- ZSVR, data year 2024: plastic participation volume 1,080,147 t, of which 764,508 t went to mechanical recycling (70.78 %). This is measured at transfer to the recycler (background, [ZSVR 27/01/2026](https://www.verpackungsregister.org/fileadmin/files/Presse/20260127_PK_2026_Packaging_recycling_fact_check.pdf)). — **déclaré**

Some Mischkunststoffe tonnages were still being clarified: "Nicht enthalten ist eine Menge von knapp 8.800 t" and "Zudem ist die Verwertung von weiteren rund 5.200 t in Klärung". — [Rheingau-Taunus-Kreis, answer to Kleine Anfrage 32/22, 11/07/2022](https://www.rheingau-taunus.de/ris/rtk/file/getfile/42020) — **déclaré**, ancien

Composition: plastic packaging 34.7 %, films 13.8 %, other plastics 25.4 %, biomass 7.4 %, metals 7.1 %, composites 6.9 % (KIT slide 8, citing Zeller et al., Chem. Ing. Tech. 93(11), 2021). — [KIT, GreenFeed 2023 slides](https://www.dbfz.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Events/Sonstiges/2023/GreenFeed/GF2023_KIT_print.pdf) — **mesuré** (one sample, pilot study)

- A city council paper (Frankenthal, 20/01/2022) states: "Kunststoffe aus der haushaltsnahen Sammlung erzeugen derzeit eher Kosten als dass Erlöse erwirtschaftet werden können". - EBS chain: the waste holder pays about 150 €/t to the EBS producer, and the EBS producer pays the cement plant about 50 €/t (ALBA background paper, file dated 11/01/2023) (background). — **déclaré / illustrative**
- EUWID, 21/12/2023: "there are a few spot-market offers to treat commercial waste for €80 per tonne"; gate fees for non-hazardous waste were "stable". - BEHG surcharge only, at 65 €/t CO₂ in 2026: "Sortierreste (MBA)" 30.84 €/t; Restabfall 39.23 €/t (ZAS Burgkirchen tariff) (background). — **contrat (tarif)**
- RDF prices "vary between − 60€ and +30€/Mg, depending on the RDF quality" (citing EU-Recycling 2019). The same KIT model assumes feedstock is "purchased for 15 €/Mg" and residue disposal at 150 €/Mg (citing EUWID 2023). — [Stallkamp et al., Waste Management 176 (2024) 105-116, p. 108 and Table 3](https://publikationen.bibliothek.kit.edu/1000167839/152125757) — RDF range: **ancien** (2019); feedstock price: model assumption
- Energy recovery of Sortierreste "ca. 100,- EUR/Mg" including transport (Lobbe, 2017) (background). — **ancien**
- EUWID headline, 25/08/2021: EEW and DSD "Plant to pre-treat roughly 200,000 tonnes of mixed plastics a year" ("mixed plastics and sorting residues"); body paywalled. — [EUWID](https://www.euwid-recycling.com/news/business/eew-and-dsd-set-up-a-joint-venture-for-the-chemical-recycling-of-mixed-plastics-250821/) — **commentaire de presse**, ancien
- EUWID, 13/02/2025: the ART Trier LVP plant disposed of PPK "bei hohen Zuzahlungspreisen" (no €/t) (background). — **commentaire de presse**

### Inferences
- The German cost benchmark for a holder of LVP residues or low-grade mixed plastics is energy recovery **paid by the holder**. From the sources above, a plausible 2023-2026 band is roughly 80-150 €/t before CO₂, plus about 30-40 €/t BEHG from 2024-2026 [calc from the cited items]. A pyrolysis plant in Germany therefore competes for feed that the holder will pay to get rid of. Pyrolysis cannot expect to buy it at a positive price unless the material has been upgraded into a polyolefin concentrate.
- Chemical recycling absorbed only about 7 kt in 2023, compared with about 1.36 Mt of plastic EBS and 2.25 Mt of plastic burnt in MVAs. The German "unrecyclable" stream is overwhelmingly a fuel market.

### Gaps
- No free absolute 2022-2026 price for DKR 350 (Mischkunststoffe), DKR 352, or LVP Sortierreste at EBS power plants, cement works or MVAs. Sources are EUWID (paywall) and bvse (deltas only). The RWI 2017 study (Rothgang et al., RWI Materialien 118), which covers the economics of energy recovery of mixed plastics, could not be fetched.
- No current national Mischkunststoffe tonnage. It sits in the detailed UBA 156/2024 tables, which were not retrieved; only the summary was readable.
- No LinkedIn or expert post with a Zuzahlung figure was found. The search budget ran out before LinkedIn-specific queries succeeded.

## Q2. Belgium: Fost Plus P+MD residues and mixed plastics, the Vanheede post-sorting contract, and Indaver Plastics2Chemicals feedstock

### Takeaway
Belgian P+MD residue is about **35,000 t/yr** across the five Fost Plus sorting centres, and Fost Plus says it is still 30-40 % recyclable. Since 2025 it has been re-sorted by Vanheede under a 5-year contract (€35 m investment). Price terms are not disclosed. Indaver's Plastics2Chemicals (P2C) plant (26 kt/yr, inaugurated 25/09/2025) depolymerises **PS** first, with polyolefins planned later. Its PS comes from Fost Plus P+MD sorting and from Citeo (at least 8,000 t/yr). **No price or gate fee is published** for P2C feedstock.

### Cited Findings
- Vanheede contract (Fost Plus press release, 19/02/2024): "around 35,000 tonnes of PMD waste per year from the five Fost Plus sorting centres"; "This is a five-year contract and an investment of 35 million euros"; starts in 2025. First step: "a new mixed industrial waste facility in Rumbeke" with "a total capacity of 100,000 tonnes". Then Vanheede's existing PMD line produces "eight separate material flows for subsequent recycling". "The residue still contains around 30 to 40% recyclable material." The release does not say where the final residue goes. — [Fost Plus / Vanheede](https://fostplus.prezly.com/vanheede-signs-contract-with-fost-plus-for-post-sorting-35000-tonnes-of-pmd-residue) — **contrat** (no price terms)
- Fost Plus 2025 results (published 09/06/2026): about 26 kg of PMD per person; "74 per cent of all plastic household packaging" recycled; 51 % processed in Belgium (12 % in 2021); domestic plastic processing rose "from 13,463 tonnes to 74,236 tonnes" over four years; the rest goes mostly to the NL, DE and FR. No residue figure. — [Fost Plus, 09/06/2026](https://fostplus.prezly.com/nearly-six-times-more-plastic-packaging-is-now-recycled-in-belgium-than-four-years-ago-xuzps1); confirmed (26.1 kg/person, 74 %, 51 %) by [Recycling Portal, 24/06/2026](https://recyclingportal.eu/?p=96401) — **déclaré**
- Fost Plus press kit (July 2023): PMD collection was to rise "van 150.000 ton naar 240.000 ton per jaar" with the new bag. Sorting into "minstens 16 fracties" at five centres: Indaver Willebroek, Val'up Ghlin, Valtris Charleroi, Sitel Liège and PreZero Evergem. Fost Plus budget 2022 "bijna 285 miljoen euro". — [Fost Plus Persdossier](https://cdn.uc.assets.prezly.com/eab6207b-641b-4595-8b5f-61899fa78dae/-/inline/no/Fost%20Plus%20-%20Persdossier.pdf) — **déclaré**
- Wrongly placed items: "Around 41,660 tonnes … each year", "13.5% of the total collected"; sorting-error cost "€40 million in 2025" (background, [Brussels Times 23/09/2026](https://www.brusselstimes.com/2329327/nearly-42000-tons-of-waste-incorrectly-disposed-as-pmd-annually-in-belgium)). — **commentaire de presse** citing Fost Plus. [calc] 41,660 / 0.135 ≈ 308,600 t of P+MD collected per year, and 35,000 t of residue ≈ 11 % of that (if both figures refer to the same year).
- Older reference: Fost Plus 2014 annual report, "PMD-residu (energierecuperatie) 22.996" t (2.9 % of total useful application), p. 6. — [Fost Plus Jaarverslag 2014](https://cdn.uc.assets.prezly.com/49ab4feb-eb2f-406d-8e03-99eeea4d75a2/-/inline/no/Jaarverslag.pdf) — **ancien** (old blue bag, before P+MD)
- Indaver P2C: feedstocks PS and PO selected "from over 100 tested feedstocks". "The majority of PS from the 5 pmd sorting centres will be chemically recycled in Antwerp." "Citeo supplies at least 8 000 tonnes of PS per year" (Indaver and Eslava together process at least 10,000 t/yr; Indaver takes 80 % for 9 years). Willebroek sorts "some 65 000 tonnes of PMD waste" per year. First plant: "a capacity of 26,000 tons per year"; target "65,000 tons per year". On PO (e.g. multilayer films), volumes "exceed the capacity of our first industrial plant", and a second PO plant is in development. **No gate fees or prices.** — [Indaver, Plastics2Chemicals](https://www.indaver.com/plastics2chemicals) — **déclaré**
- Packaging Europe, 30/09/2025: P2C is "producing chemically recycled raw materials"; "anticipated annual capacity of 26kT"; investment "€105 million"; "long-term contracts with companies like Fost Plus and Citeo"; customers INEOS Styrolution, Trinseo, Borealis, TotalEnergies and Neste. — [Packaging Europe](https://packagingeurope.com/news/first-raw-materials-shipped-from-indavers-thermal-depolymerization-plant/13420.article) — **commentaire de presse**
- INEOS Styrolution, 25/09/2025: the plant was inaugurated that day and "The first truckloads of recycled styrene monomer (SM) have arrived" in Antwerp. No volumes or terms. — [INEOS Styrolution](https://www.ineos.com/nl/bedrijven/ineos-styrolution/Nieuws/ineos-styrolution-marks-milestone-with-first-commercial-delivery-of-recycled-styrene-monomer-from-indaver/) — **déclaré**
- Flemish government VEKP progress annex (28/10/2022): Ecoo Houthalen "capaciteit uitgebreid tot 32.000 ton voor gemengde kunststof in 2021". Indaver Willebroek "totale capaciteit van 60.000 ton"; PreZero Gent "totale input capaciteit van 78. 000 ton". Chemical recycling could recover "nog 60 000 ton kunststoffen" more. — [Vlaamse Regering, bijlage VEKP-voortgangsrapport 2022](https://themis-test.vlaanderen.be/files/6356a54ff33e1b000c000b5c/download) — **déclaré**, ancien
- BlueAlp/Renasci (Oostende, BE) is covered in Q6.
- Flemish incineration levy 32 €/t; combustible landfill 134.22 €/t (2026) (background). — **contrat (tarif/taxe)**

### Inferences
- The Belgian residue tonnage, about 35 kt/yr for 11 million inhabitants (about 3 kg/inhabitant/yr [calc]), is small because P+MD excludes paper. Fost Plus is now paying to re-sort it rather than burn it. In Belgium, the plastic-rich part of residue is therefore being taken out by a mechanical post-sorting contract before any pyrolysis or fuel outlet sees it.
- Indaver P2C is a PS-first plant. Its feedstock is a **sorted PS fraction** under long-term PRO contracts (Fost Plus, Citeo), not residue. It is not a proxy for an outlet that takes unsorted refus.

### Gaps
- Commercial terms of the Vanheede contract (€/t, who pays for the final residue) are not disclosed. The EUWID article is paywalled after the teaser.
- No Fost Plus activity report for 2023-2025 with a P+MD residue tonnage, mixed-plastics ("other plastics") tonnage or their destination was found. No price or gate fee for Indaver P2C feedstock was found. No OVAM 2023-2025 residue statistics were retrieved.

## Q3. Netherlands: Verpact data on PMD residue and mixed plastics, NL "mix"/DKR 350 prices, and incineration price including tax and CO₂ levy

### Takeaway
Verpact 2024: of 534 kt of plastic packaging placed on the market, **369 kt were sent to recyclers, of which 106 kt were lost ("uitval")**, leaving 262 kt recycled (49 %). About 29 % of what is sent to recyclers is lost [calc]. Verpact describes many plastic fractions as worth "weinig tot niets", and for some it "legt er zelfs geld bij". **No public NL price for "mix"/DKR 350 was found.** A Verpact chain-model price shows a **kunststofresidu deduction of about 35-47 €/t**. The incineration benchmark is the AVI gate fee (example: AVU budget tariff €165/t for 2026) plus the waste tax (€40.85/t in 2026) and, from 2026, the CO₂ levy.

### Cited Findings
- Verpact, results 2024 (report form explanation, Sept 2025): plastic placed on the market excluding reuse "534" kt; "Verzonden aan recyclers" 369 kt; "Uitval" 106 kt; "Recycling" 262 kt; recycling rate 49 % (pp. 4, 19, 25-28). — [Verpact, Toelichting verslagleggingsformulier 2024](https://www.verpact.nl/sites/default/files/2025-09/VRP_toelichting_verslagform_2024_13.pdf) — **déclaré**. [calc] 106/369 ≈ 29 % loss after delivery to recyclers.
- Verpact financing explainer (Dec 2025): "Veel plastics leveren weinig tot niets op", and Verpact "legt er zelfs geld bij" for some plastic types. — [Verpact, uitleg financiering](https://www.verpact.nl/sites/default/files/2025-12/uitleg_financiering_recycling_verpakkingen_verpact.pdf) — **déclaré** (qualitative)
- Verpact PMD explainer (May 2024): in 2022 Verpact paid "101 miljoen euro aan gemeenten" and "172 miljoen euro aan vergoeding aan voornamelijk sorteer- en recyclebedrijven" (273 M€ total). Collection fee in 2023: 245 €/t (source separation), 14 €/t (post-separation). Contamination limit "15%". Loads above 15 % "worden verbrand en krijgt de gemeente geen vergoeding". — [Verpact, Hoe werkt de inzameling en recycling van PMD-afval](https://verpact.nl/sites/default/files/2024-05/Hoe%20werkt%20de%20inzameling%20en%20recycling%20van%20PMD-afval%20in%20Nederland.pdf) — **déclaré / contrat**
- Verpact fee factsheet (11/07/2024): collection fee for source separation €261.07/t (2024); post-separation €8.90-14.74/t. Chain-management model ("Ketenregiemodel"): base fee €656/t; "Verrekenprijs" €168.98, €146.20 and €119.16/t, **net of a kunststofresidu deduction of €46.56, €39.14 and €35.26/t** (year columns not labelled; probably 2022-2024). — [Verpact Factsheet Vergoedingen](https://verpact.nl/sites/default/files/2024-07/Factsheet%20Vergoedingen_20240711.pdf) — **contrat**
- Hengelo (letter of 10/07/2024): 2,047 t of PMD accepted by Verpact in 2023 in the "tussencategorie" (15-35 % contamination). Mechanical post-sorting of contaminated PMD costs "vergelijkbaar" (comparable) to direct incineration; no figures. — [Gemeente Hengelo](https://hengelo.nl/bestanden/documenten/bestuur/schriftelijke-vragen/2024/2024-07-09_Antwoord_College_vragen_LokaalHengelo_na-sorteren%20vervuild%20PMD.pdf) — **déclaré**
- ISPT/Circular Plastics Initiative (30/05/2023): the sorted Dutch DKR 350 stream was "not suitable" for the pyrolysis tested, because "The pyrolysis product contains too many halogens and oxygen to serve as feedstock for new plastics". — [ISPT](https://ispt.eu/news/cpi-tackles-pmd-recycling-pyrolyse-is-not-effective/) — **mesuré** (lab/pilot)
- Petrík et al. (WM&R, online 31/12/2024): DKR-350 is "not recycled or has low recycling value due to high contamination and property variability"; "229 kt of household packaging waste was incinerated with energy recovery in 2017". — [Petrík et al. 2024](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/0734242X241306605) — **déclaré**, ancien for 2017
- Background (not re-read in this session): Dutch mixed plastics are a large share of sorted output (Brouwer: "mix" 47 of 105 Gg of sorted products). ICIS reported that in 2021 NL RDF reject bales "continue to trade in negative territory".
- Taxes (background): waste tax on landfill and incineration €39.70/t (2025) and **€40.85/t (2026)** ([IPLO](https://iplo.nl/thema/bodem/nieuws-bodem/2025/afvalstoffenbelasting-omhoog-per-1-januari-2026/)); CO₂ levy on incinerators €87.9 (2025) and €103.7 (2026) per t CO₂ above dispensation rights ([Rijksoverheid](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/afval/verhoging-afvalstoffenbelasting)). — **contrat (taxe)**
- AVU (Utrecht waste authority) letter of 08/01/2025: "Begrotingstarief restafval (2026): € 165" per tonne. The letter says the waste tax and CO₂ levy come on top of processing tariffs, and the CO₂ levy applies for the first time in 2026. Whether the €165 includes these levies is not stated explicitly. — [AVU, aanbiedingsbrief ontwerpbegroting 2026](https://secure-woerden.gemeenteoplossingen.nl/Stukken/Bijlage-1-Aanbiedingsbrief-bij-de-Ontwerpbegroting-2026-AVU-D25170225.pdf) — **déclaré (budget)**
- Attero runs "the largest MBT in the world with an annual capacity of 800,000 tonnes" and recovers film and PE/PP from residual waste to DKR specifications. "The EPR-fees currently only cover 10% of the cost". — [Attero](https://www.attero.nl/en/news/plastics-recycling-from-rdf/) — **déclaré** (undated)

### Inferences
- The kunststofresidu deduction of about 35-47 €/t in the Verpact chain model is the only public Dutch figure that puts a cost on PMD sorting residue. It is per tonne of collected plastic, not per tonne of residue.
- A Dutch holder of residue or contaminated PMD faces the AVI gate fee (example €165/t budget for 2026) plus €40.85/t tax and the new CO₂ levy. This makes the Netherlands one of the more expensive incineration outlets in the region, which supports a positive gate fee for any alternative outlet.

### Gaps
- No NL "mix"/DKR 350 bale price for 2022-2026 was found. Verpact does not publish market values per fraction (background), and ICIS/Platts are paywalled.
- No Verpact/Afvalfonds tonnage for PMD sorting residue or for the mix fraction in 2022-2025 was found. Municipal tariff memos (Leusden, Ouder-Amstel, Bergen NH) returned "Toegang geweigerd".

## Q4. United Kingdom: letsrecycle price series for low-grade plastics (2024-2026), PRN, residual-waste plastic and pyrolysis feedstock deals

### Takeaway
The UK has the best free monthly series. **MRF film** (bags and wrappings from MRFs) traded at **−£120 to £0/t in 2024, −£110 to −£50/t in 2025, and −£110 to −£25/t in 2026** (September 2026: −£75 to −£25/t). Pots, tubs and trays (PTT) were negative through 2025 (−£50 to +£20/t) and turned positive in 2026 (September 2026: £0 to £65/t). ### Inferences
- None of the named plants discloses whether it pays for or charges on its feedstock. The indirect evidence points one way: zero or negative bale prices for low-grade fractions (UK MRF film, Spanish PM and film, NWE reject bales per ICIS), holder-paid energy-recovery costs (DE, NL, UK), and operators citing "receiving" prices (Quantafuel). **The realistic assumption for a French business plan is that a pyrolysis plant receives a gate fee for unsorted refus**, bounded above by the holder's alternative (incineration or CSR plus tax). For a pre-sorted polyolefin concentrate meeting Platts-type specs, the plant may instead pay a small positive price.
- Plant failures and postponements in 2025-2026 (Quantafuel, Plastic Energy's holding company, OMV, Mura's unclear start-up) mean that offtake-side value (pyrolysis oil) was not sufficient even where feed was cheap or free. Business-plan sensitivity should rest on the oil price and the PPWR/recycled-content mandate, not on feedstock cost.
