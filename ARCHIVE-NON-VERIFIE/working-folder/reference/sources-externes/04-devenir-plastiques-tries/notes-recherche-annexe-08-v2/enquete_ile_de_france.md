## Q1. Plastic in OMR treated by Île-de-France syndicates: tonnages, destinations, prices (activity reports, budgets, deliberations)

### Takeaway
IDF OMR is about **3.2 Mt/yr (2024)**. Almost all of it goes to the 18 IDF incinerators, which have 4.28 Mt of authorised capacity. **No IDF syndicate publishes a plastic share of its OMR** in any document read. With the national MODECOM plastic share [bg], the plastic in IDF OMR is in the order of **~0.5 Mt/yr** [calcul, modélisé]. About 0.26 Mt of that is in SYCTOM's OMr. The documents read give treatment costs of **~50–110 €/t for incineration** plus TGAP of 15 €/t. They give no price for plastic recovered from OMR, because none is recovered.

### Inferences
- **Plastic in IDF OMR ≈ 3.2 Mt × 15.8 % ≈ 0.51 Mt/yr** [calcul; inputs ORDIF 2024 + MODECOM [bg]; *modélisé*]. For SYCTOM, 1.67 Mt × 15.8 % ≈ **0.26 Mt** [calcul]. Paris's 2024 OMR is 655 kt, of which 43 % is mis-sorted packaging of all materials. That is consistent with a 10–20 % plastic share but does not establish one.
- Destination: about 95 % of IDF OMR goes to UVE. Landfill takes only a small residual, mostly during UVE shutdowns: Paris 18.7 kt in 2024, SIGIDURS 4 kt in 2023 [inference from the sources above].
- Economics: plastic in OMR has **negative value**. It currently costs **~50–110 €HT/t** to incinerate in IDF (SMITOM Lombric 49.65 €/t technical cost; SYCTOM ~109 €/t external, mapping uncertain), plus TGAP of 15 €/t. These are **commingled OMR prices**; plastic is never isolated from OMR in IDF.
- Capacity pressure: SYCTOM needs 270 kt of external treatment in 2026. Incinerators run at 80 % on DMA alone and also take DAE. A new long-term concession (SIETREM/Paprec, 26 years) is now in place. **IDF incinerators therefore remain hungry for high-PCI fractions.** Any project that pulls plastic out of OMR competes with incinerators locked into long-term concessions [inference].

### Gaps
- **No IDF-specific OMR characterisation with a plastic share** was found for any of the 12 syndicates.
- No **compte administratif or budget line** was found for SYCTOM. Searches for "recettes de vente de matériaux" and "dépenses de traitement des refus" returned nothing for SYCTOM.
- No usable 2022–2026 reports were found for SITREVA (only RA 2020 exists, not read: https://www.eure-et-loir.gouv.fr/contenu/telechargement/45198/268745/file/7.%20sitreva_ra_2020_bdef.pdf), SIMACUR, GPSO, SIETREM (apart from the UVE notice) or SMITOM Nord 77 (apart from a newsletter).

## Q2. Plastic in sorting rejects of the 18 sorting centres serving IDF (refus 146,368 t in 2024, 98 % incinerated)

### Takeaway
ORDIF's **146,368 t of refus (27 %) in 2024, 98 % incinerated** is confirmed. Syndicate-level refus rates for 2023–2025 sit between **25 % and 31 %**, so the regional figure is credible. Almost no syndicate publishes a refus **tonnage**. None publishes the **plastic content of refus**. The plastic fraction of IDF refus is therefore only modelled: **~18 kt/yr of identifiable plastic packaging**, probably 25–45 kt/yr including non-packaging plastics [calcul, modélisé, fragile]. The only refus disposal cost found is **SYCTOM 129.4 €/t (2024)**, paid to send "anomalies" to UVE.

Any alternative outlet would have to be built into SYCTOM's contracts with sorting operators or carved out from them [inference].

## Q3. Plastic in DAE sorting rejects and déchèterie tout-venant in IDF

### Takeaway
Plans and observatories give **mixed DAE at 2.89 Mt (2022)**, with **65 % sent to sorting** and **57 % material recovery** against a 69 % target. IDF landfill still takes about **2 Mt/yr of non-DMA non-hazardous waste**, most of it DAE and sorting residues, against a legal ceiling that drops to 1.30 Mt (2025) and 1.04 Mt (2031). **No figure for DAE-sorting refus tonnage or plastic content in IDF was found.** Déchèterie **tout-venant ≈ 0.31 Mt (2024)** [calcul] and door-to-door encombrants ≈ 0.27 Mt (2024). These are mixed bulky streams whose plastic share is unpublished.

### Cited Findings

  - Mixed DAE "2022 : 2,89 Mt" (p. 19), down from 3.41 Mt (2014) and 3.35 Mt (2018).
  - Mixed DAE sent to a sorting chain "2022 : 65%" (p. 19).

  - Region-wide: "129 centres de tri et/ou de transit (dont quais de transfert des DMA), dont 17 centres de tri des DMA" (p. 13). By difference, about 112 sites are DAE or other sorting and transit sites [calcul], with no capacities given.

## Q6. Synthesis: realistic tonnages, current destinations and prices by stream (IDF)

### Takeaway
The best-documented IDF plastic-rich residual flows, from largest to smallest plastic mass:
1. Plastic in OMR: ~0.5 Mt plastic, modelled.
2. Plastic in DAE and encombrants residues currently landfilled (within ~2 Mt landfilled); plastic share unmeasured.
3. Plastic in collecte sélective refus: ~18–45 kt within 146 kt.

### Inferences
- For a plastic-to-fuel project, the **most accessible IDF feedstock** today is collecte sélective refus (146 kt, publicly priced at ~130 €/t to UVE). Its absolute plastic content is modest, and it is locked into SYCTOM sorting contracts (PSE option in BOAMP 25-80033) and long-term UVE concessions.
- The **largest unexploited plastic mass under regulatory pressure** is DAE and encombrants residues still landfilled (~2 Mt total, which must roughly halve by 2031). From 2031 it will be contested by Thermo-sur-Seine.
- Plastic in OMR is the largest plastic mass, but it is only accessible through mechanical-biological pre-treatment, which IDF syndicates have not proposed in any document read.
