## Q1. Overall mass balance: how much household plastic packaging enters separate collection, and how much is lost at each step (a)–(e)?

### Takeaway
Three public numbers frame the household balance. About **1.17–1.22 Mt** of plastic packaging is placed on the market each year. About **473 kt** of sorted plastic bales were taken back by repreneurs in 2024. Only **324 kt** (2024) and **346 kt** (2025) count as "effectivement recyclé". So about **150 kt per year (≈31 % of the bales)** leaves the chain between bale pick-up and the EU calculation point [calcul]. That loss is spread across non-target material in the bales, moisture and dirt, and losses at the recycler and the surtri centre. A further **≈85–120 kt** of plastic packaging is lost earlier, in sorting-centre refus [calcul]. The two numbers cover different steps, so they add rather than overlap. Neither is measured directly; both are derived from declared Citeo figures.

### Cited Findings
**Placed on market and recycled (household, Citeo perimeter)**
- 2025: 1,174 kt placed on the market ("gisement contribuant", ARC), against 1,165 kt in 2024; **346 kt recycled in 2025, against 324 kt in 2024 (+7 %)**. Citeo attributes the rise to the "Flux Développement" and better capture of films, without a tonnage by flux. `déclaré` — [Citeo, Rapport d'activité 2025, p. 12 and p. 29](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rapport-dactivite-2025_Citeo.pdf)
- Since 2023, Citeo counts tonnes "effectivement recyclées" under the harmonised EU method, not quantities accepted at the recycler's input, which "mechanically lowers rates" (footnote 3). `déclaré` — [Citeo, Fiche chiffres clés 2023](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/241223_CITEO_Fiche_Chiffres_cles-2023_Notre_Impact.pdf)
- Plastic packaging recycling rate: 24.5 % in 2022 ([Citeo, Chiffres clés 2022](https://notre-impact-2024.citeo.com/240418_Chiffres_cles_2022_FR_WEB.pdf), `déclaré`) and 27 % in 2023, with the "gisement" described as "stable" ([CB News/AFP, 25/11/2024](https://www.cbnews.fr/node/88845), `secondaire`). The same AFP piece says 120,000 t more plastic was recycled between 2022 and 2023. That figure is inconsistent with a 2.5-point rise on about 1.19 Mt and is probably a misreading, so do not use it.
- Older series: "244 000 t in 2012 → 379 500 t in 2022" recycled (+55 %). This series is probably on the old "accepted at input" basis (see Inferences). `déclaré` — [Citeo, Cahier Collecte sélective, nov. 2024, p. 14](https://bo.citeo.com/sites/default/files/2024-11/CITEO_Cahier_Collecte%20s%C3%A9lective_nov%202024.pdf)

**Bales taken back (repris)**
- 2024: "Plastiques : 473 033 t", "+ 8,8 %" against 2023 (column "Tonnage 2024, OSCAR (sept. 2025)"); gisement contribuant (ARC 2024) 1,224 kt (slide 5). `déclaré` — [Citeo, Observatoire de la Reprise 2024, 06/10/2025, slide 5](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/20251006_Observatoire_reprise_2024_VDEF.pdf)
- 2023: "Plastiques : 434 580 t", +12.4 % against 2022; gisement contribuant (ARC 2023) 1,190 kt, "-0,1 %" against the real 2022 gisement (slide 5). `déclaré` — [Citeo, Observatoire de la Reprise 2023, 30/09/2024, slide 5](https://bo.citeo.com/sites/default/files/2024-10/20240930_Observatoire_reprise_2023_VDEF.pdf)
- Valorplast's director general (Catherine Klein, RDV IFPEN, 02/07/2025): « En 2024, environ 467 000 tonnes d'emballages plastiques devraient être collectées et valorisées »; volumes sent to recycling grow 8–12 % per year. This is consistent with the 473 kt above, so it is a national figure, not Valorplast's own. `secondaire` (event synthesis) — [IFPEN, synthèse RDV IFPEN « Recyclage des plastiques », 02/07/2025](https://www.ifpenergiesnouvelles.fr/print/pdf/node/1904)
- Inconsistency: the gisement is given as 1,224 kt (Observatoire, ARC 2024) and as 1,165 kt for 2024 (Rapport d'activité 2025). The perimeter or vintage probably differs. Both are `déclaré`.

**Sorting-centre refus (b), from background, re-used**
- Refus were 23.0 % of all selective collection in 2022, about 800 kt ([Citeo, Cahier Collecte sélective, p. 18-19](https://bo.citeo.com/sites/default/files/2024-11/CITEO_Cahier_Collecte%20s%C3%A9lective_nov%202024.pdf), `déclaré`). OCAPEM gives 750 kt, with 12 % of identifiable plastic packaging (7.5 % rigid, 4.5 % flexible) (background, `non-vérifié`). Citeo's ECT 2020 report estimates that "15 à 20 % du gisement plastiques entrant dans les centres de tri" ends in refus (background, `modélisé`).
- Films: « 55 % des films sont captés en centres de tri » (Catherine Klein, Valorplast, 02/07/2025). `secondaire` — [IFPEN synthèse](https://www.ifpenergiesnouvelles.fr/print/pdf/node/1904)

**Non-target in bales (d), measured 2025 (average target content against the standard)**
- PETC « Mix » 94.8 % (std 98 %); PETF « Mix » 97.2 % (98 %); PE/PP/PS 85.6 % (98 %); **Films PE 80.8 % (95 %)**; PETC bouteilles et flacons 96.9 % (98 %); PEHD/PP 86.7 % (95 %); Flux développement films PE/PP 88.8 % (90 %); Flux développement plastique rigide en mélange 92.3 % (90 %); flux rigides à trier 92 % (90 %) (pp. 27-33). `mesuré` — [Citeo, Observatoire de la qualité 2025](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/OBQ-2025-VDEF.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Bale-to-recycled gap, 2024: 473 kt − 324 kt = ≈149 kt, i.e. ≈31.5 % of bales taken back** [calcul: Observatoire 2024 slide 5; Rapport d'activité 2025 p. 29]. Caveats: (i) the Observatoire tonnage excludes DROM-COM "sauf mention contraire", and the recycled perimeter is not stated; (ii) under EU rules the "recycled" figure may be measured at the recycler or estimated with average loss rates, so this gap is a **declared-minus-declared difference, not a measurement**. It combines (d) non-target material and moisture in bales, and (e) recycler and surtri losses.
- The trend points the same way. Bales taken back rose 8.8 % in 2024, but recycled tonnage rose only about 1 % (≈321 kt in 2023 [calcul: 27 % × 1,190 kt] → 324 kt). This fits the growing low-grade flows (films, flux développement) having much lower yields. That is an inference, not a published statement.
- 2022 cross-check: bales taken back were ≈386.6 kt [calcul: 434,580 / 1.124]. That is close to the 379.5 kt "recycled" in the old series, which supports reading the old series as "accepted at the recycler". The new-method 2022 figure would be ≈292 kt [calcul: 24.5 % × ≈1,191 kt], so the 2022 gap was ≈95 kt (≈25 % of bales).
- **Plastic lost in sorting-centre refus (b): ≈85–120 kt/yr.** Two routes give similar ranges: 750–800 kt × 12 % = **90–96 kt** of identifiable plastic packaging, which is a floor because non-packaging plastics and nested objects sit in the "autres"; and, if 15–20 % of entering plastic goes to refus and ≈473 kt leaves as bales, **83–118 kt** [calcul: 473/(1−0.15…0.20) − 473]. Both inputs are estimates.
- **Order of magnitude for the whole household chain (2024):** ≈1.2 Mt placed on the market → ≈560–590 kt of plastic reaching sorting centres [calcul, includes non-target] → ≈473 kt in bales → ≈324 kt counted as recycled. The ≈600 kt not reaching the yellow bin (in OMR, littered or composted) is outside this question's scope.
- Non-target share in bales ranges from about 3 % (PET foncé) to 19 % (films PE). At an assumed average of about 10 % of bale mass, non-target material would be roughly 45–50 kt of the 149 kt gap [calcul, assumption: weights by flux are not published].

### Gaps
- No public tonnage of bales by flux (PET clair, PET foncé, PEHD/PP, films, flux développement, PS) for 2023–2025. The Observatoire de la Reprise slides 42–51 hold them only as charts the extraction could not read; read the PDF visually.
- No plastic-only refus tonnage for France after 2022, and no measured plastic content of refus with a published method (OCAPEM is `non-vérifié`).
- The precise measurement point and method behind Citeo's "effectivement recyclé" (measured at recyclers or estimated with average loss rates) are not published.

## Q2. (a) How much household plastic packaging placed on the market has no recycling route at all?

### Takeaway
The official 2021 indicator classes **19 % of household plastic packaging as having no recycling route expected by 2025**: 17 % PVC, complexes and other resins, plus 2 % recyclable resins carrying sorting or recycling disruptors. Another **15 % sat in "filières en développement"** (rigid PET, PS, flexible PP). On ≈1.19 Mt, that is **≈225 kt with no route and ≈180 kt in development** [calcul]. Citeo's 2025 figure of "75 % recyclable" implies **≈290 kt not recyclable** [calcul]. The 100 % eco-modulation malus covered roughly **8–23 kt (most likely ≈20 kt)** in 2025 [calcul]. Under the extended sorting instructions these packages still go in the yellow bin, so they end up in refus or as non-target material in bales.

### Cited Findings
- ADEME, indicators of the 3R decree (household, 2021, excluding beverage cartons, p. 14): **66 % recyclable** (50 % SYDEREP data: clear PET bottles and flasks, PE, PP, dark/coloured PET bottles and flasks, flexible PE; 16 % Citeo estimate: rigid PE and PP); **15 % "filière en développement"** (11 % SYDEREP: rigid PET and rigid PS; 4 % Citeo estimate: flexible PP); **19 % "pas de filière attendue d'ici 2025"** (17 % "PVC et complexes ou autres résines"; 2 % recyclable resin with disruptors, malus). Method: tonnage with an operational recycling route and no disruptors ÷ total household plastic packaging tonnage, from SYDEREP declarations plus Citeo estimates (p. 10). This measures recyclability potential, not effective recycling (p. 8). `déclaré`/`modélisé` — [ADEME, Bilan indicateurs décret 3R – Synthèse](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/documents/ADEME_Bilan%20indicateurs%20d%C3%A9cret%203R-Synthese.pdf)
- The interim 3R review (June 2025) repeats « ~66 % » recyclable (2021) and "19 %" of single-use plastic packaging with no recycling route (p. 13), citing a federations' roadmap assessment. It also gives a plastic packaging recycling rate of 25.2 % in 2022 (Eurostat, p. 1). `déclaré` — [Bilan intermédiaire Stratégie 3R, juin 2025](https://www.ecologie.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/documents/Bilan%20interm%C3%A9diaire_Strat%C3%A9gie%203R_%20juin%202025.pdf)
- Stratégie 3R, original text (p. 8): « Environ 2,4 millions de tonnes d'emballages en plastique sont mis en marché en France chaque année », about half household. P. 10: « environ 65 % des emballages en plastique ménager disposent d'une filière de recyclage opérationnelles »; for « 15 % des résines (PS/PSE, PP souple, pots et barquettes PET operculés ou multicouche) » routes are in development; « Les 20 % restant (autres résines et emballages complexes ou contenant des éléments perturbateurs du recyclage) » « ne disposent pas de perspectives de recyclage effectif d'ici 2025 ». No year is given for the data. `déclaré` — [Stratégie 3R pour les emballages en plastique à usage unique](https://www.ecologique-solidaire.gouv.fr/sites/default/files/Consulter%20la%20Strat%C3%A9gie%203R%20pour%20les%20emballages%20en%20plastique%20%C3%A0%20usage%20unique.pdf)
- Citeo: plastic recyclability "75 % in 2025", up from 65 % (+10 points since 2020) (p. 5). `déclaré` — [Citeo, Rapport d'activité 2025](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rapport-dactivite-2025_Citeo.pdf). Also « La recyclabilité des plastiques a doublé en dix ans, passant de 35 à 75 % » — [Citeo, Plan plastique et concertation nationale, 16/06/2026](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Plan-plastique-et-concertation-nationale.pdf), `déclaré`.
- OPECST (June 2023), note 83: plastic packaging judged non-recyclable: **35 %** (no year or source given). `secondaire` — [OPECST, Note n° 39 « Le recyclage des plastiques »](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/media/16/organes/delegations-offices-cec/opecst/pdf/note-39-recyclage-plastique)
- Valorplast (C. Klein, 02/07/2025): « 65 % des plastiques sont recyclés via des filières mécaniques matures »; 15 % go to developing routes (PS, films; chemical recycling about to start with TotalEnergies and Indaver); 20 % "still need innovation"; « 80 % des emballages collectés sont techniquement recyclables ». `secondaire` — [IFPEN synthèse](https://www.ifpenergiesnouvelles.fr/print/pdf/node/1904)
- Xerfi (17/11/2025): "on average, 65 % of plastic waste is recyclable (mainly PET and PEhd), and 15 % falls into emerging streams" (paraphrase from the extraction). `secondaire` — [Xerfi, P. **Eco-modulation as indirect evidence**
- 2025 malus totals (all packaging): malus 10 % **7.0 M€** (0.7 M€ in 2024); malus 100 % **12.5 M€** (15.1 M€ in 2024); total 19.7 M€ (p. 14). No breakdown by material or tonnage. `déclaré` — [Citeo, Rapport d'activité 2025, p. 14](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rapport-dactivite-2025_Citeo.pdf)
- In the 2025 tariff, the **malus 100 %** cases are all plastic: PET opaque with more than 4 % mineral fillers (p. 35); rigid PET combined with aluminium, PVC or silicone of density > 1 (p. 36); PVC bottles and flasks (p. 37), described as « Emballages plastiques dans les règles nationales de tri mais non recyclables et non valorisables »; dark rigid plastics not detectable by optical sorting (p. 38). 2025 base rates in ct€/kg: PET clair bouteille 53.52; PET foncé 56.19; PE/PP bottles and rigid 58.87; rigid PET non-bottle 64.22; flexible PE 58.87; flexible PP 64.22; rigid PS 64.22; **"Emballage complexe ou autres résines hors PVC" (6.6.2) 107.03**; PVC (6.7) 160.55 (p. 22). - 2026 tariff: no malus targets "packaging without a recycling route" as such. The **non-recyclable tariff lines themselves act as the penalty**: "Emballage complexe ou autres résines hors PVC" 140.01 ct€/kg and PVC 210.01 ct€/kg, against 70–84 ct€/kg for recyclable resins (p. 27). P. 26 groups complexes and other non-PVC resins as packaging "with no recycling route, recovered for energy", and PVC as "non recyclable, non valorisable". The PVC bottle malus was removed because the PVC tariff « fait office de malus » (p. 9). No tonnages are given. `déclaré` — [Citeo, Guide du tarif des emballages ménagers 2026](https://ecd-bo.citeo.com/ecd2/medias/citeo/public/fr/guide-du-tarif-des-emballages-menagers-2026.pdf)

### Inferences
- **No recycling route (2021 basis): ≈226 kt** [calcul: 19 % × 1,190 kt, using the ARC 2023 gisement because no 2021 tonnage was read]. That splits into ≈202 kt PVC, complexes and other resins, and ≈24 kt recyclable resins with disruptors. A further **≈179 kt** sat in developing routes [calcul: 15 % × 1,190 kt].
- **Citeo 2025: ≈294 kt "not recyclable"** [calcul: (1 − 0.75) × 1,174 kt]. 66 % + 15 % = 81 % in 2021 against 75 % in 2025 suggests the definitions differ (some developing routes may be counted as not yet recyclable). The 19–25 % range should be reported with its source.
- **Malus 100 % tonnage, 2025: ≈8–23 kt** [calcul: 12.5 M€ ÷ base rates from 1.6055 €/kg (PVC) to 0.5352 €/kg (PET clair)]. If the malus falls mostly on PET, PE and PP rigids at 0.56–0.64 €/kg, the most likely value is **≈20 kt** [calcul]. The malus applies "sur le tarif de l'UVC", which includes a per-unit component, so the tonnage is slightly overstated. The 10 % malus (7.0 M€) cannot be converted, because it also covers non-plastic formats (beverages ≤ 0.5 L, promotional bundles).
- The bulk of non-recyclable plastic (complexes, other resins, PVC) **does not pay a malus**; it pays a higher base rate under tariff line 6.6.2 or 6.7. Citeo's declared tonnage on those lines is the decisive figure, and it is not published.
- Under the extended sorting instructions (ECT), all plastic packaging goes in the yellow bin. These ≈200–300 kt therefore enter separate collection to the extent people sort them, and come out as sorting refus or as non-target material in bales. Citeo's December 2025 infographic names "EMBALLAGES SANS FILIÈRE DE RECYCLAGE" as one of three refus categories (background).

### Gaps
- No tonnage by tariff line (6.6.2 complexes and other resins; 6.7 PVC) or tonnage carrying a malus in the Citeo, Léko or Adelphe reports read. Léko and Adelphe annual reports were not read (search budget exhausted).
- COTREP publishes guidance only (no tonnages, in the sources read).
- The ADEME synthesis has a figure 3 with professional (non-household) tonnages that did not extract.

## Q3. (c) Valorplast and the low-grade flows: tonnages by flux, outlets, countries, energy recovery

### Takeaway
Valorplast handles about **45 % of household plastic bales** (172 kt of ≈387 kt in 2022 [calcul]). It and Citeo say **74–76 % of plastic is recycled in France and 24–26 % in neighbouring Europe, with nothing outside Europe**. No public source gives Valorplast's tonnage by flux. For the low-grade flows, the best published figures are Citeo's 2022 tender forecasts: **≈108 kt in 2025** of flux développement sub-flows (films ≈48 kt, coloured and opaque PET ≈36 kt, PET pots and trays ≈17 kt, PS ≈7 kt). The **contracted outlets for films are at least half chemical recycling**: Paprec/TotalEnergies at Grandpuits (pyrolysis, started March 2026) and Indaver P2C in Antwerp. The rest goes to Machaon. Machaon's mechanical film recycling yields about 50 %, and half of its residue is burned in cement kilns. For PS, a ≈100 kt gisement faces a 10 kt contracted outlet, 80 % of it Indaver P2C. No source read states where films, PS and trays went before these outlets opened (2023–2026). The contract floor price of 0 €/t (background) and the missing published prices for films and flux développement keep that hidden.
