## Q1. Île-de-France: tonnages of collecte sélective and refus de tri, by syndicate and sorting centre; refus rates; destinations; ORDIF reports

### Takeaway
ORDIF's 2024 regional review (latest edition) is the controlling figure. In 2024 Île-de-France collected **539,795 t** of emballages + papiers (excluding glass). The refus rate was **27 %**, or **146,368 t** of refus de tri, and **98 %** of it went to incineration. SYCTOM publishes facility-level tonnages for its five sorting centres, about 43 % of the regional flow [calc]. It reports a refus share (25.2 % in 2024, 27 % "anomalies" in 2025) but no refus tonnage. Other syndicates publish only fragments: rates, or tonnages without rates.

### Inferences
- ORDIF's 146,368 t ÷ 539,795 t = 27.1 % [calc], consistent with the stated 27 %. At 98 % incineration, about 143,000 t of IDF collecte sélective refus went to UVE in 2024, and only about 3,000 t went elsewhere [calc]. **IDF collecte sélective refus is almost entirely an incineration flow, not a landfill or CSR flow.**
- SYCTOM share of the regional collecte sélective: 230,701 / 539,795 ≈ **43 %** (2024) [calc].
- SYCTOM refus order of magnitude: 25.2 % × 230,701 t ≈ **58 kt (2024)**; 27 % × 231,239 t ≈ **62 kt (2025)** [calc]. Caution: SYCTOM's percentages come from **characterisations of bin contents** ("anomalies", "composition"), not from weighed refus leaving the centres. They are proxies, not measured refus tonnages.
- Paris arithmetic: 106,364 − 77,695 = 28,669 t [calc]. The "réorientées vers l'incinération" figure is therefore simply collected minus entered-in-Paris-centres. It most likely covers déclassements (whole loads diverted before sorting), possibly also flows sent to non-Paris centres, rather than in-plant refus. 28,669 / 106,364 = 27 %, but the page says 36 %; 28,669 / 77,695 = 37 %. **The page's 36 % uses an inconsistent base.** Its statement that "77,677 t were recycled" ignores in-plant refus, so recycled tonnage is overstated.
- IDF refus (146 kt, 2024) ≈ 18 % of the national 800 kt of collecte sélective refus (2022, Citeo, [bg]) [calc, different years].
- **Plastic content of IDF refus** (very fragile): OCAPEM's single-centre characterisation gives 12 % identified plastic packaging in refus ([bg]). 146 kt × 12 % ≈ **18 kt/yr of identifiable plastic packaging** in IDF collecte sélective refus [calc], plus an unknown share of non-packaging plastics in the "autres" fraction. SYCTOM's 11 % plastic packaging share applies to **bin contents**, not refus. It is not a refus composition.
- The SITRU figure of 13,241 t "refus de tri" against 14,930 t of collecte sélective (89 %) cannot be collecte sélective refus alone. It most likely includes refus from encombrants or other sorting. Treat it as ambiguous.

## Q2. Sorting centres serving Île-de-France: capacity, operator; Sepur surtri centre at Thiverval-Grignon

### Takeaway
ORDIF counts **18 sorting centres** serving IDF collectivities, with **678,200 t/yr** authorised capacity. **14 are inside IDF (526,200 t/yr).** 16 of the 18 are under public maîtrise d'ouvrage, and operation is delegated to Paprec, Veolia, Suez, Urbaser and Sepur. Facility-level capacity and operator are confirmed for the 5 SYCTOM centres, SIREDOM/Serivel, SIGIDURS (operator only) and the Paprec Blanc-Mesnil centre (operator only). The Sepur surtri centre (30 kt/yr of rigid "flux développement" plastics, from end 2026) has published inputs and outputs but **no published reject figure**.

### Inferences
- The five SYCTOM centres total 217,200 t of authorised capacity [calc]. The Paris XVII fire removes 45,000 t, leaving 172,200 t [calc]. Against SYCTOM's 231 kt of collecte sélective in 2025, roughly 40-60 kt/yr must have gone to external or private centres in 2025 [calc, rough]. Romainville (59,167 t vs 55,000 t) and Paris XVII (46,406 t vs 45,000 t) were above authorised capacity in 2024.
- The 4 centres outside IDF serving IDF collectivities total 678,200 − 526,200 = 152,000 t of capacity [calc]. One is Dreux (28). The others are not named.
- Surtri rejects: if typical surtri losses were known, 30 kt × loss rate would give the Thiverval-Grignon reject flow. No source gives this rate (see Gaps). A rigid-plastics surtri reject stream would be **highly plastic and very close to Paris**, making it the most interesting IDF-located plastic-rich stream for a pyrolysis project. This is an inference, not a measured fact.

## Q3. France national: refus de tri tonnage 2020-2025; DAE sorting residues; the FNADE "2 738 kt" perimeter; CSR 2021-2025; CSR plants and boilers in or near IDF

### Takeaway
The only national collecte sélective refus tonnage remains Citeo's **800 kt (2022)** [bg]. No 2023-2025 national figure was found; Citeo's 2025 annual report gives none. **FNADE's "2 738 kt" is not a total refus figure.** It is the refus de tri **landfilled** in 2020 per ADEME's ITOM survey, all origins mixed, as part of 17.4 Mt landfilled. It therefore mainly reflects DAE and other sorting residues and excludes refus that were incinerated. CSR consumption rose from 370 kt (2021) to 510 kt (2023) against about 2.5 Mt of theoretical capacity. No 2024-2025 national CSR figure, and no CSR plant or boiler in IDF, was found.

### Cited Findings
- **FNADE perimeter, verified wording**: "Selon l'enquête ITOM 2020, 17,4 Mt étaient stockées en 2020 dont 2 738 kt de refus de tri." (section 3, p. 3 or 4; the page markers are ambiguous). The document gives no split by gisement. — [FNADE/SN2E, synthèse modèle économique CSR](https://www.fnade.org/ressources/documents/source/1/4855-FNADE-SN2E-Synthese-modele-economique-CSR-VDEF.pdf) [INDUSTRY]
- ADEME confirms the 17.4 Mt: non-hazardous, non-inert waste landfilled in 2020 "y compris refus de traitement"; 14.5 Mt incinerated with energy recovery in 2020 (p. 4). — [ADEME, Déchets chiffres-clés, édition 2023 (copy on ecologic-france.com)](https://www.ecologic-france.com/images/medias/document/21269/dechetsdeeechiffres-cles-2023ademe.pdf) [OFFICIAL STAT]
- The regional ITOM 2020 (Normandy) groups the category as **"Refus de tri / compostage"**: 21 % of ISDND inputs and 5 % of UVE inputs (750,800 t) in Normandy (pp. 10, 12). Normandy had 36 sorting centres: 8 DMA and **28 DAE**. Incoming flows were about 115,700 t of household collecte sélective and **about 340,000 t of DAE**; 11 of the 28 DAE sites did not report (p. 4). Average collecte sélective refus was about 28 % (p. 5). — [Bilan ITOM 2020 Normandie](https://neci.normandie.fr/sites/default/files/2024-04/Bilan%20ITOM%202020.pdf) [OFFICIAL STAT, regional]
- National collecte sélective refus: "800 000 tonnes de refus issus des centres de tri de collecte sélective en 2022". Rates: 21.5 % (2020), 22.3 % (2021), 23.0 % (2022). — [bg: Citeo Cahier Collecte sélective nov. 2024, p. 19](https://bo.citeo.com/sites/default/files/2024-11/CITEO_Cahier_Collecte%20s%C3%A9lective_nov%202024.pdf)
- Citeo Rapport d'activité 2025 gives **no refus tonnage or rate**. It only reports a support line for "valorisation énergétique des emballages dans les refus issus des centres de tri" of 12.3 M€ (2024) and 12.0 M€ (2025) (p. 28). — [Citeo RA 2025](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Rapport-dactivite-2025_Citeo.pdf) [OPERATOR]
- The Citeo key-figures sheet for 2023 gives no refus figure. It says plastic packaging recycled rose 15 % vs 2022, with the note "Ce calcul prend en compte non pas les quantités acceptées par les recycleurs à l'entrée du process". — [Citeo Fiche chiffres clés 2023](https://www.citeo.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/241223_CITEO_Fiche_Chiffres_cles-2023_Notre_Impact.pdf)
- **CSR consumption 2021**: 370,000 t consumed in France, of which 60,000 t in dedicated boilers and 310,000 t in cement kilns. About 91,000 t were exported to European cement plants (FEDEREC data). Boiler sizes modelled: 50 MW ≈ 96 kt/yr of CSR; 19.9 MW ≈ 38 kt/yr (p. 5). — [FNADE/SN2E](https://www.fnade.org/ressources/documents/source/1/4855-FNADE-SN2E-Synthese-modele-economique-CSR-VDEF.pdf) [INDUSTRY]
- **CSR consumption 2023**: "En 2023, 510 000 tonnes de CSR ont été consommés", "dont seulement 61 000 tonnes par des chaufferies implantées sur le territoire national"; "les capacités théoriques de production de CSR en France ont atteint 2,5 millions de tonnes"; "les sites ne sont actuellement qu'à 20 % de leur capacité". Amendment I-980 rect. bis, PLF 2026, 26/11/2025, MM. Cambier, Billon, Courtial and Louault; withdrawn. **No source is cited for the CSR figures.** — [Sénat, amendement I-980](https://www.senat.fr/enseance/2025-2026/138/Amdt_I-980.html) [PARLIAMENT]
- Regional example, 2024 (provisional, ADEME/TEO survey, edition Sept. 2025): Pays de la Loire produced 74,000 t of CSR, from 7 preparation platforms in operation, with 2 CSR boilers, one commissioned December 2024. — [TEO Pays de la Loire, fiche filière valorisation énergétique 2024](https://www.pays-de-la-loire.developpement-durable.gouv.fr/IMG/pdf/fiche_filiere_valo_nrj_dechets_2024_vf.pdf) [OFFICIAL STAT, regional]

### Inferences
- **The 2,738 kt is landfilled refus (2020), all origins.** In 2020, 85-86 % of collecte sélective refus in extended-sorting territories went to energy recovery ([bg] Citeo ECT 2020), and IDF sends 98 % of its refus to incineration. So only a minor share of the 2,738 kt can be household collecte sélective refus. Most must be **DAE sorting residues, encombrants/déchèterie sorting residues and other "refus de traitement"** [inference]. The Normandy ITOM category label "Refus de tri / compostage" suggests the national figure may also include composting refus [inference]. **It must not be compared to, or added to, Citeo's 800 kt without checking the ITOM definitions.**
- Implication for gisement sizing: the **largest plastic-bearing residual mass is likely DAE and encombrants sorting residues, much of it still landfilled**, rather than household collecte sélective refus (≈800 kt, mostly incinerated) [inference]. Its plastic content is unmeasured in the sources read.
- CSR utilisation: 510 / 2,500 ≈ 20 % [calc], consistent with the Sénat text. CSR consumption grew about 38 % from 2021 to 2023 [calc].

### Gaps
- National collecte sélective refus tonnage and rate for **2023, 2024 and 2025**: not found (Citeo RA 2025 and 2023 key figures are silent).
- **National ITOM 2020 report** (ADEME/SINOE) not read directly; the 2,738 kt is verified only as quoted by FNADE. Its exact category definition (refus de tri vs refus de traitement, compost refus, DAE vs DMA) remains unverified. The newer ITOM 2022 edition, if published, was not found.

The FEDEREC press release on DAE incineration (https://federrec.com/FEDEREC/documents/CPIncinerationdesDechetsNonDangereuxdesActivitesEconomiquesV25595.pdf) was blocked by a permission timeout and not read.
- National CSR production/consumption **2024-2025**: not found. SNCR data not found. The Sénat 2023 figure is unsourced.
- **CSR preparation plants and CSR boilers in or around IDF**: none identified in sources read. Gap.
