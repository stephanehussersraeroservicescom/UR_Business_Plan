# Chapter 3 Annex: Equipment Configuration Analysis

## Equipment Configuration Comparison: 2×100T vs 1×200T

This analysis compares two equipment configurations for the CDG site to determine optimal deployment strategy.

---

## Configuration Options

### Option A: 2 × 100 tons/day units (Dual Configuration)

**Total Daily Capacity**: 200 tons/day combined
**Annual Throughput**: 73,000 tons/year (assuming 365-day operation with 5% downtime each unit)

### Option B: 1 × 200 tons/day unit (Single Large Unit)

**Total Daily Capacity**: 200 tons/day
**Annual Throughput**: 73,000 tons/year (assuming 365-day operation with 5% downtime)

---

## 1. CAPACITY AND OPERATIONAL FLEXIBILITY

### Option A: 2 × 100T Units

**Advantages**:
- ✅ **Redundancy**: One unit can continue operating while other undergoes maintenance
- ✅ **Operational flexibility**: Can process different feedstock types simultaneously
  - Example: Unit 1 processes MSW, Unit 2 processes plastic-rich airport waste
  - Optimizes product quality (separate oil streams from plastic vs. mixed MSW)
- ✅ **Phased deployment**: Install first unit, prove concept, then add second unit
- ✅ **Risk mitigation**: Technical failure of one unit = 50% capacity retained (not 0%)
- ✅ **Load balancing**: Better adaptation to waste supply fluctuations

**Disadvantages**:
- ⚠️ **Higher staffing**: Requires personnel for both units (though some shared)
- ⚠️ **More complex logistics**: Two feed systems, two control rooms
- ⚠️ **Larger footprint**: Two separate processing lines

**Effective Annual Capacity**:
- **Scenario 1 - Coordinated maintenance**: 70,000-72,000 tons/year (96-99% utilization)
  - When Unit 1 down for maintenance (10 days), Unit 2 operates at 100 tons/day
  - When Unit 2 down (10 days), Unit 1 operates at 100 tons/day
  - Remaining 345 days: Both operate at 200 tons/day combined
  - Annual: (345 × 200) + (20 × 100) = 71,000 tons/year

---

### Option B: 1 × 200T Unit

**Advantages**:
- ✅ **Lower CAPEX**: Single larger unit typically 15-20% cheaper than two smaller units
- ✅ **Economies of scale**: Higher efficiency per ton processed
- ✅ **Simpler operations**: One control system, one maintenance schedule
- ✅ **Smaller staffing**: ~20% fewer personnel vs. dual configuration

**Disadvantages**:
- ❌ **No redundancy**: Maintenance or breakdown = 100% capacity offline
- ❌ **All-or-nothing**: Cannot optimize for different feedstock types
- ❌ **Higher risk**: Single point of failure
- ❌ **Less flexible**: Fixed processing mode

**Effective Annual Capacity**:
- **Scenario - Scheduled maintenance**: 67,000-69,000 tons/year (92-95% utilization)
  - Maintenance downtime: 20-25 days/year
  - Operating days: 340-345 days
  - Annual: 340 × 200 = 68,000 tons/year

## 4. REVENUE POTENTIAL AND PRODUCT OPTIMIZATION

### Option A: 2 × 100T Units - Feedstock Optimization Strategy

**Key Advantage**: Ability to process different feedstock types separately

**Scenario: Optimized Dual Processing**

**Unit 1 - Plastic-Rich Feedstock** (35,000 tons/year):
- Sources: Airport packaging, logistics EPS, industrial plastics
- Oil yield: **40%** (high plastic content)
- Product revenue: €280/ton (oil €200/ton + biochar €30/ton + metals €50/ton)
- Gate fee: €110/ton
- **Total revenue: €390/ton**

**Unit 2 - Mixed MSW** (35,000 tons/year):
- Sources: Municipal solid waste, mixed commercial waste
- Oil yield: **30%** (standard MSW)
- Product revenue: €190/ton (oil €150/ton + biochar €20/ton + metals €20/ton)
- Gate fee: €90/ton
- **Total revenue: €280/ton**

**Combined Annual Revenue**:
- Unit 1: 35,000 × €390 = €13.65M
- Unit 2: 35,000 × €280 = €9.80M
- **Total: €23.45M/year**

**Constraint**: All feedstock processed together (blended)

**Blended Feedstock** (68,000 tons/year):

- Product revenue: €235/ton (oil €175/ton + biochar €25/ton + metals €35/ton)

## 8. STRATEGIC CONSIDERATIONS

### Phased Deployment Strategy (Favors 2 × 100T)

**Phase 1 - Year 1-2**: Install first 100T unit
- Prove technology at CDG location
- Secure waste supply contracts
- Establish refinery offtake relationships
- CAPEX: €13-18M
- Revenue: €11-12M/year from single unit

**Phase 2 - Year 3-4**: Add second 100T unit
- Expand to full 200T capacity
- Optimize feedstock mix across units
- Additional CAPEX: €11-15M
- Revenue: Scale to €23M+/year

**Advantages**:
- ✅ De-risks technology deployment
- ✅ Spreads capital requirements
- ✅ Allows market validation before full commitment
- ✅ Easier to secure financing (lower initial ask)

**Disadvantages**:
- ⚠️ Slower to full revenue potential
- ⚠️ Higher total CAPEX
- ⚠️ Two permitting/construction cycles

---

### Single Large Deployment Strategy (Favors 1 × 200T)

**Phase 1 - Year 1-2**: Install 200T unit
- Full capacity immediately
- Maximum economies of scale
- CAPEX: €18-24M
- Revenue: €22.8M/year immediately at full operation

**Advantages**:
- ✅ Fastest to full revenue
- ✅ Lowest total CAPEX
- ✅ Lowest OPEX per ton
- ✅ Single permitting/construction cycle

**Disadvantages**:
- ⚠️ Higher upfront capital requirement

## 10. RECOMMENDATIONS

### For CDG Pilot Project: **Recommended Configuration Depends on Strategy**

#### **If priority is RISK MITIGATION and PROVEN DEPLOYMENT**: Choose 2 × 100T
- Start with single 100T unit (€13-18M CAPEX)
- Prove concept for 12-18 months
- Add second unit once validated
- **Best for**: First Urban Rig deployment in France, investor risk aversion

#### **If priority is FINANCIAL EFFICIENCY and SPEED TO SCALE**: Choose 1 × 200T
- Deploy full 200T capacity immediately (€18-24M CAPEX)
- Maximize economies of scale
- Fastest payback (0.9-1.2 years)
- **Best for**: Established Urban Rig track record, confident waste supply, strong financing

---

### For CDG Specifically: **RECOMMENDATION = 2 × 100T (Phased)**

**Rationale**:
1. **First mover in France**: Needs proof of concept before scaling
2. **Complex stakeholder environment**: ADP, TotalEnergies, municipalities all need to see success first
3. **Regulatory validation**: Prove ICPE compliance, air quality, operations before doubling capacity
4. **Market validation**: Secure waste contracts incrementally
5. **Financing strategy**: Easier to finance €13-18M initial vs. €18-24M upfront
6. **Feedstock optimization**: CDG has diverse waste streams (airport, MSW, industrial) - benefit from dedicated processing

**Implementation**:
- **Unit 1 (Year 1-2)**: Focus on airport waste + Île-de-France MSW blend
- **Unit 2 (Year 3-4)**: Add plastic-rich logistics/industrial waste processing, optimize mix

## 12. CONCLUSION

### Financial Efficiency Winner: **1 × 200T**
- Lower CAPEX (€21M vs €28M)
- Lower OPEX per ton (€34 vs €42)
- Faster breakeven (13 months vs 26 months)
- Higher 5-year cumulative cash flow (+€14.8M)

### Operational Resilience Winner: **2 × 100T**
- Redundancy during maintenance/failures
- Feedstock optimization flexibility
- Phased deployment reduces risk
- Better capacity utilization (97% vs 93%)

### **RECOMMENDED FOR CDG: 2 × 100T (Phased Deployment)**

**Final Rationale**:
Despite lower financial efficiency, the **2 × 100T phased approach is recommended for CDG** because:
1. **This is the first Urban Rig deployment in France** - proof of concept is critical
2. **Complex regulatory environment** - demonstrate compliance before full commitment
3. **Stakeholder confidence** - ADP, TotalEnergies, municipalities need to see results
4. **Financing accessibility** - €13-18M initial ask vs €21M is materially easier
5. **Market risk mitigation** - validate waste supply and product offtake before full scale
6. **Operational learning curve** - French labor, regulations, stakeholders are new territory

**Once proven**: Future deployments (Lyon, Marseille, etc.) can use 1 × 200T configuration to maximize financial efficiency.
