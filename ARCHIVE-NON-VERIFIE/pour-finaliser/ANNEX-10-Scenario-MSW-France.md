# Annex 10 — Scenario Economics: Municipal Solid Waste (France)

**Purpose:** Full financial model for a URC-2000 deployment processing French MSW. Combines the feedstock profile from Annex 5, pricing from Annex 3, equipment configuration from Annex 9, and country-level parameters from Annex 4A.

**Date:** March 2026
**Status:** PLACEHOLDER — to be completed when scenario modelling phase begins.

---

## Scope

This annex will contain:

1. **Feedstock parameters** — MODECOM 2017 composition (Annex 5), 23.7% plastic-like content, 140 T/day effective throughput
2. **Revenue projections** — Gate fees (€114/T blended), oil at Medium scenario (€641/T weighted), biochar at Tier 2 (€100/T), metals, carbon credits (€50/T CO$_2$)
3. **CAPEX** — Per Annex 9 §9.5.1 MSW configuration (~€65.4M)
4. **OPEX** — Maintenance, personnel (15 FTE, €83K loaded), electricity, purchased heating oil, contingency
5. **Energy balance** — Off-gas utilization with purchased heating oil for reactor heating; all char sold as product (V2 model)
6. **P\&L, cash flow, and payback** — 15-year projection
7. **Sensitivity analysis** — Oil price, gate fee, throughput, OPEX

**Reference:** Current financial model (UR-Financial-Model-General.xlsx) contains a working version at 30% plastic content. This annex will adapt it to the 23.7% MODECOM composition.
