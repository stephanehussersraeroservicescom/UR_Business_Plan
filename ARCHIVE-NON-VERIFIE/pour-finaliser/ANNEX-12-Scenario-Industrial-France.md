# Annex 12 — Scenario Economics: Industrial Residues (France)

**Purpose:** Full financial model for a URC-2000 deployment processing industrial and commercial waste (DAE). Combines the feedstock profile from Annex 7, pricing from Annex 3, equipment configuration from Annex 9, and country-level parameters from Annex 4A.

**Date:** March 2026
**Status:** PLACEHOLDER — to be completed when scenario modelling phase begins.

---

## Scope

This annex will contain:

1. **Feedstock parameters** — DAE composition (Annex 7), variable plastic content by industrial sector
2. **Revenue projections** — Gate fees (industrial rates), oil, biochar, metals, carbon credits
3. **CAPEX** — Per Annex 9 §9.5.1 industrial configuration (~€64.9M)
4. **OPEX** — Adapted to industrial waste characteristics
5. **Energy balance** — Variable by feedstock composition
6. **P\&L, cash flow, and payback** — 15-year projection
7. **Sensitivity analysis**
8. **Industrial-specific considerations** — Contract structures with waste generators, feedstock variability, multi-source blending
