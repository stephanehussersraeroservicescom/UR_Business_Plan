# 11. Legal and Compliance

## 11.1 Corporate Structure and Governance

### Legal Entity

**Company Form**: Société par Actions Simplifiée (SAS)
- Flexible governance structure
- Limited liability for shareholders
- Suitable for joint ventures
- French commercial law compliance

**Registration**:
- Registered with Registre du Commerce et des Sociétés (RCS)
- Trade registry (Greffe du Tribunal de Commerce)
- SIRET and SIREN numbers
- VAT registration

**Corporate Documents**:
- Articles of Association (Statuts)
- Shareholders' Agreement (governing JV relationship)
- Board procedures and decision-making rules
- Delegation of authority matrix

### Shareholding and Governance

**Ownership**:
- Urban Rig Co. Ltd: 50%
- French partner: 50%

**Board Composition**:
- 2 directors from each partner
- Optional: 1 independent director
- Decisions requiring unanimity (reserved matters):
  - Major capital expenditures
  - Technology changes
  - Disposal of assets
  - Partner exit or new partner admission
  - Dividend policy

**Management Appointment**:
- General Manager appointed by Board
- Requires approval of both partners for key executives

### Liability and Indemnification

**Limited Liability**:
- Shareholders liable only for unpaid capital contributions
- Corporate veil protects partners from operational liabilities

**Insurance Requirements**:
- Comprehensive liability coverage
- Directors and Officers (D&O) insurance
- Professional indemnity for management

**Indemnification**:
- Company indemnifies directors and officers for actions taken in good faith
- Exceptions: Fraud, gross negligence, criminal acts

## 11.2 Environmental Permits and Compliance

### ICPE (Installations Classées pour la Protection de l'Environnement)

**Classification**:
- **Primary code**: 2770 (traitement de déchets - waste treatment)
- **Possible additional codes**:
  - 1XXX series (if combustion or energy production)
  - 2XXX series (other waste-related activities)

**Authorization Level**:
- **Autorisation** (most stringent regime, requires Prefecture approval)
- Not "Enregistrement" or "Déclaration" due to facility size and waste processing nature

**ICPE Application Components**:
1. **Administrative information**: Company details, site location, legal status
2. **Technical description**: Detailed process description, equipment specifications, capacity
3. **Environmental impact study** (Étude d'impact):
   - Baseline environmental conditions
   - Predicted impacts (air, water, soil, noise, biodiversity)
   - Mitigation measures
   - Monitoring plan
4. **Risk study** (Étude de dangers):
   - Hazard identification
   - Risk scenarios (fire, explosion, release)
   - Safety measures and emergency response
5. **Remediation and closure plan**: Decommissioning procedures, site restoration
6. **Non-technical summary**: Public-facing summary document

**Public Inquiry**:
- Minimum 30 days (typically 1-2 months)
- Public can submit comments and concerns
- Public hearings may be held
- Company must respond to all substantial comments

**Timeline**:
- Application preparation: 3-6 months
- Regulatory review: 9-12 months (including public inquiry)
- **Total**: 12-18 months typical

**Ongoing Compliance**:
- **Annual declaration**: Emissions, waste processed, incidents
- **Self-monitoring**: Continuous emissions monitoring, periodic sampling
- **Regulatory inspections**: DREAL inspections (announced and unannounced)
- **Incident reporting**: Immediate notification of accidents, exceedances, or complaints

### Water and Wastewater Permits

**Water Use Authorization**:
- Required if >1,000 m³/year extraction or connection
- Application through local water authority
- Urban Rig uses minimal water (closed-loop recycling)

**Wastewater Discharge Permit**:
- Required if discharging to sewer or surface water
- Discharge limits set by local authority and water agency
- Target: Zero discharge (closed-loop system)
- If discharge necessary: Regular monitoring and reporting

### Air Emissions

**Integrated with ICPE Authorization**:
- Emission limits set in ICPE decree
- Continuous emissions monitoring system (CEMS) required

**Parameters Monitored**:
- CO, CO₂ (carbon oxides)
- NOx (nitrogen oxides)
- SOx (sulfur oxides)
- Particulate matter (PM10, PM2.5)
- VOCs (volatile organic compounds)
- Dioxins and furans (periodic testing)
- Heavy metals (periodic testing)

**Reporting**:
- Real-time data transmission to authorities (for continuous monitors)
- Quarterly summary reports
- Annual emissions declaration (GEREP - Gestion Électronique du Registre des Émissions Polluantes)

**Compliance**:
- Emission limits typically set at 10-20% of BAT (Best Available Techniques) levels
- Urban Rig design meets or exceeds limits
- Exceedances must be reported immediately with corrective action plan

### Waste Management Permits

**Waste Processing Authorization**:
- Covered under ICPE authorization
- Specifies:
  - Types of waste accepted (EWC codes - European Waste Codes)
  - Maximum quantities (200 tons/day, ~73,000 tons/year)
  - Storage duration and conditions

**Waste Transport**:
- Waste suppliers must have proper transport permits
- Traceability system (BSD - Bordereau de Suivi des Déchets or electronic equivalent)
- Records maintained for 3+ years

**Product Classification**:
- Recovered oil, carbon, and metals must be properly classified
- If considered waste: Requires proper waste codes and handling
- If considered products: Simpler regulatory regime
- Goal: Demonstrate "end-of-waste" criteria to classify outputs as products, not waste

## 11.3 Labor and Employment Law

### French Labor Code Compliance

**Employment Contracts**:
- Contrat à Durée Indéterminée (CDI - permanent contract) for most employees
- Possible Contrat à Durée Déterminée (CDD - fixed-term) for project or seasonal work
- Written contracts mandatory
- Trial period (période d'essai) allowed per legal/collective agreement rules

**Working Time**:
- 35-hour workweek (legal standard)
- Shift work (24/7 operations) requires specific arrangements
- Overtime limits and compensation per Labor Code
- Rest periods and breaks mandatory

**Collective Agreements (Convention Collective)**:
- Likely applicable: Chemical industry or waste management sector agreement
- Provides minimum wages, benefits, and working conditions beyond Labor Code
- Must be applied to all employees

### Occupational Health and Safety

**Regulatory Framework**:
- Code du Travail (Labor Code) provisions
- DUERP (Document Unique d'Évaluation des Risques Professionnels):
  - Mandatory risk assessment document
  - Updated annually and after any significant change
  - Identifies workplace hazards and mitigation measures

**Safety Committee**:
- CSE (Comité Social et Économique) required for 11+ employees
- Includes employee representatives
- Meets regularly on health, safety, and working conditions

**Training Requirements**:
- Initial safety training for all employees
- Specialized training for high-risk tasks
- Annual refreshers
- Emergency response training and drills

**Medical Surveillance**:
- Médecine du travail (occupational health service)
- Regular health checks for employees
- Fitness-for-work assessments
- Exposure monitoring if handling hazardous substances

### Employee Benefits and Social Contributions

**Mandatory Contributions** (employer + employee):
- Social security (health, retirement, unemployment): ~60-65% of gross salary
- Pension contributions
- Professional training levy

**Benefits**:
- Paid leave: Minimum 5 weeks/year
- Public holidays
- Sick leave and maternity/paternity leave per law
- Possible supplemental insurance and benefits per collective agreement

## 11.4 Commercial Contracts

### Waste Supply Agreements

**Key Terms**:
- **Parties**: Valorea and waste supplier (municipality, private collector, CDG Airport)
- **Term**: Long-term (5-10 years) with renewal options
- **Volume commitment**: Minimum and maximum tons per month/year
- **Tipping fee**: Price per ton, escalation formula (CPI, fixed %)
- **Waste specifications**: Acceptable waste types, exclusions, contamination limits
- **Delivery terms**: Schedule, logistics, truck access, unloading procedures
- **Quality control**: Right to inspect and reject non-conforming waste
- **Liability**: Responsibility for hazardous materials, damage, contamination
- **Termination**: Conditions for early termination, notice periods

**Legal Considerations**:
- Compliance with French procurement rules if contracting with public entities
- Competitive tender may be required for public contracts
- Anti-trust compliance (no market allocation or price-fixing with competitors)

### Product Offtake Agreements

**Sustainable Oil Offtake**:
- **Buyer**: Refinery, chemical producer, or industrial fuel user
- **Term**: 3-5 years with renewal options
- **Volume**: Minimum purchase commitment (e.g., 18,000-21,000 tons/year)
- **Pricing**: Formula linked to crude oil benchmark (Brent, Dated Brent, or equivalent) or fuel oil index
- **Quality specifications**: API gravity, sulfur content, moisture, heating value
- **Delivery**: Ex-works (buyer collects) or delivered (Valorea arranges transport)
- **Payment terms**: Net 30-60 days
- **Force majeure**: Excuses non-performance due to unforeseeable events

**Carbon Black Offtake**:
- **Multiple buyers** likely (cement, agriculture, activated carbon)
- Shorter-term agreements or spot sales
- Pricing: Fixed or market-linked
- Quality specs: Particle size, moisture, ash content, heating value

**Metals Offtake**:
- **Scrap metal dealers**: Typically spot market sales
- Pricing at market rates on delivery

**Legal Considerations**:
- Ensure products meet buyer specifications and regulatory requirements
- Liability limits for product quality claims
- Insurance for product liability

### Technology License and Support Agreement

**Licensor**: Urban Rig Co. Ltd
**Licensee**: Valorea

**Key Terms**:
- **License grant**: Right to use Urban Rig patented technology for the facility
- **Territory**: Specific site near CDG (not exclusive broader rights)
- **Term**: Duration of facility operation
- **License fee**: Likely included in equipment purchase price or ongoing royalty
- **Technical support**: 24/7 hotline, remote diagnostics, periodic site visits
- **Support fee**: Annual support contract (~€50-100K/year)
- **Training**: Initial training included, ongoing training at additional cost
- **Improvements**: Access to technology upgrades and improvements
- **Confidentiality**: Protect Urban Rig proprietary information
- **Warranties**: Equipment performance guarantees
- **Liability**: Limits on Urban Rig liability for operational issues

### Construction and Equipment Contracts

**EPC (Engineering, Procurement, Construction) Contractor**:
- May use single EPC contractor or multiple specialized contractors
- Fixed-price contract preferred to control costs
- Performance guarantees and liquidated damages for delays
- Retention (5-10% held back until successful commissioning)

**Urban Rig Equipment Supply**:
- Separate contract with Urban Rig Co. Ltd
- Price, delivery schedule, installation support, warranties
- Performance guarantees (throughput, energy efficiency, product yield)

## 11.5 Insurance and Liability

### Required Insurance Policies

**Property Insurance**:
- All-risk coverage for equipment, buildings, inventory
- Insured value: Replacement cost
- Deductibles and sub-limits

**General Liability**:
- Coverage for third-party bodily injury or property damage
- Minimum coverage: €5-10 million per occurrence
- Includes premises liability and operations liability

**Environmental Liability**:
- Pollution and remediation coverage
- Gradual and sudden releases
- On-site and off-site damage
- Minimum coverage: €3-5 million

**Business Interruption**:
- Covers lost revenue during operational downtime
- Triggered by insured property damage
- Waiting period (deductible in time, e.g., 14 days)
- Coverage period: 12-18 months

**Workers' Compensation**:
- Mandatory for all employees
- Covers workplace injuries and occupational diseases

**Directors and Officers (D&O)**:
- Protects management from personal liability for company decisions
- Coverage: €2-5 million

**Professional Indemnity** (if providing consulting or advisory services):
- Errors and omissions coverage

**Product Liability**:
- Coverage for claims related to products sold (oil, carbon, metals)
- Important if products cause damage to buyers or end-users

### Liability Limitations

**Contract Terms**:
- Limit liability in commercial contracts where possible
- Exclude consequential damages
- Cap liability at contract value or insured amounts

**Corporate Structure**:
- SAS provides limited liability protection
- Personal assets of shareholders protected (except fraud, gross negligence)

## 11.7 Data Protection and Privacy

### GDPR Compliance (General Data Protection Regulation)

**Personal Data**:
- Employee data (HR records, health information)
- Visitor data (site access logs)
- Customer/supplier contact information

**Requirements**:
- Lawful basis for data processing
- Transparent privacy policies
- Data security measures
- Data retention limits
- Rights of data subjects (access, correction, deletion)
- Data breach notification procedures

**DPO (Data Protection Officer)**:
- May be required if large-scale processing of sensitive data
- Likely not required for Valorea (small employee count)
- Can designate responsible person for GDPR compliance

### Confidential Business Information

**Protection**:
- Non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) with partners, contractors, advisors
- Employee confidentiality obligations in contracts
- Access controls for sensitive information

## 11.8 Compliance Management System

### Compliance Program

**Compliance Officer**:
- HSE Manager serves as primary compliance lead
- Supported by management team

**Compliance Calendar**:
- Track all regulatory deadlines (permits, reports, inspections)
- Automated reminders

**Training**:
- Compliance training for all employees (annual)
- Specialized training for roles with specific regulatory duties

**Audits**:
- Internal compliance audits (annual)
- Third-party audits (periodic, or as required by permits)
- Management review of audit findings

### Regulatory Updates

**Monitoring**:
- Subscribe to regulatory updates (DREAL, ADEME, industry associations)
- Legal counsel monitors legislative and regulatory changes
- Impact assessment for new regulations

**Adaptation**:
- Update procedures and systems as needed
- Communicate changes to relevant personnel
- Document compliance with new requirements

### Incident and Complaint Management

**Incident Reporting**:
- Internal reporting system for safety, environmental, or compliance incidents
- Investigation procedures
- Corrective and preventive actions
- Regulatory reporting as required (ICPE, labor inspectorate, etc.)

**Community Complaints**:
- Complaint hotline and email
- Log all complaints with response and resolution
- Quarterly review of complaint trends
- Communication with complainants
