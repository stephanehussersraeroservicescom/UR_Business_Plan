## 9.3 Revenue Streams

### Revenue Overview

Valorea has **three primary revenue streams**:

1. **Tipping fees** (waste acceptance)
2. **Product sales** (oil, carbon, metals)
3. **Environmental credits** (potential future revenue)

---

### 1. Tipping Fees

**Pricing**:
- Must be competitive with alternatives (landfill, incineration)
- Île-de-France tipping fees (typical):
  - Landfill: €120-180/ton
  - Incineration: €80-150/ton
  - **Urban Rig target**: €80-120/ton

**Volume**:
- Design capacity: 200 tons/day
- Annual (90% uptime): ~70,000 tons/year
- Year 1 (ramp-up): 60,000 tons (estimate)

**Revenue**:
- At €100/ton average tipping fee:
  - Year 1: €6.0M
  - Steady state: €7.0M/year

**Sensitivity**:
- ±€20/ton = ±€1.4M/year revenue impact

---

### 2. Product Sales

#### A. Sustainable Oil

**Yield**:
- ~85% of plastic content, ~30-40% of total MSW by weight
- Assume 30% oil yield from mixed MSW
- 70,000 tons MSW → ~21,000 tons oil/year

**Pricing**:
- Linked to crude oil and fuel oil markets
- Assume €400-700/ton (wide range depending on quality and oil prices)
- Base case: €500/ton

**Revenue**:
- €10.5M/year (at €500/ton)

**Sensitivity**:
- ±€100/ton = ±€2.1M/year

---

#### B. Carbon Black / Biochar

**Yield**:
- ~15-20% of input weight
- 70,000 tons MSW → ~12,000 tons carbon/year

**Pricing**:
- Fuel substitute (cement kilns): €80-150/ton
- Agricultural biochar (certified): €200-400/ton
- Blended estimate: €120/ton

**Revenue**:
- €1.4M/year (at €120/ton)

**Sensitivity**:
- ±€50/ton = ±€600K/year

---

#### C. Recovered Metals

**Yield**:
- ~5-10% of MSW input
- 70,000 tons MSW → ~5,000 tons metals/year

**Pricing**:
- Mixed scrap metal: €200-400/ton
- Estimate: €300/ton

**Revenue**:
- €1.5M/year (at €300/ton)

**Sensitivity**:
- Market-driven; assume ±20% volatility

---

### 3. Environmental Credits (Future Potential)

**Potential Sources**:
- **Carbon credits**: 80% GHG reduction = ~25,000 tons CO₂e/year avoided
  - At €30-80/ton CO₂e → €750K - €2M/year
- **Renewable fuel certificates**: If oil qualifies
- **Biochar carbon sequestration credits**: If carbon used for soil amendment

**Status**: Not included in base case financial model (conservative approach)

**Upside Potential**: €0.5-2M/year if markets develop favorably

### Return on Investment (ROI) and Payback

**Assumptions** (to be refined with actual capex):
- Total CAPEX: €20M (illustrative)
- Debt: 50% (€10M) at 4% interest
- Equity: 50% (€10M)

**Debt Service**:
- 15-year loan at 4% interest: ~€900K/year

**Cash Available for Equity** (after debt service):
- ~€16M - €0.9M = €15M/year

**Simple Payback on Equity**:
- €10M equity / €15M annual cash return = **<1 year** (unrealistically fast - indicates very strong economics)

**More Realistic Analysis** (accounting for taxes, reserves, etc.):
- After-tax cash flow to equity: ~€10-12M/year
- Payback: **~1-2 years**
- **IRR (Internal Rate of Return)**: >>50% (exceptionally strong)

**Sensitivity**: Even with 30% lower revenue or 30% higher costs, project remains highly attractive.

---

### Break-Even Analysis

**Fixed Costs** (annual): ~€2.5M (personnel, insurance, fixed maintenance)
**Variable Costs** (per ton): ~€7-10 (utilities, variable maintenance, handling)

**Break-Even Throughput** (tipping fees only, no product sales):
- At €100/ton tipping fee: ~30,000 tons/year (42% of capacity)
- **With product sales**: Break-even at <20% of capacity

**Conclusion**: Very low break-even point provides significant downside protection.

- Attractive financial returns (reduces equity hurdle)
