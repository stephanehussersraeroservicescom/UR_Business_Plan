# 3. Project Description: CDG Urban Rig Deployment

## 3.1 Project Overview

### 3.1.1 Project Mission

Deploy Urban Rig's continuous pyrolysis technology at **Charles de Gaulle Airport (CDG)** industrial zone to process **70,000+ tons/year** of waste from multiple sources, converting it into valuable products while eliminating dependency on incineration and landfilling.

**Strategic Positioning**:
- **First Urban Rig deployment in France**
- **Flagship European demonstration** of waste-to-value circular economy
- **Integration with TotalEnergies SAF program** - closing the loop from CDG waste to CDG jet fuel
- **Multi-stakeholder collaboration**: Aéroports de Paris (ADP), TotalEnergies, Île-de-France municipalities

---

### 3.1.2 Project Scope

**Location**: Industrial zone near Charles de Gaulle Airport (Roissy-en-France, Tremblay-en-France, or similar)

**Equipment Configuration**: **2 × 100 tons/day continuous pyrolysis units** (phased deployment)

**Total Capacity**: 200 tons/day combined (73,000 tons/year at 365-day operation)

**Investment**: €24.5-33M total CAPEX (phased: €13-18M Unit 1, €11-15M Unit 2)

**Timeline**:
- **Phase 1 (Year 1-2)**: Unit 1 deployment - 100 tons/day capacity
- **Phase 2 (Year 3-4)**: Unit 2 deployment - expand to full 200 tons/day

**Revenue Model**: Dual revenue stream
- **Tipping fees**: €80-150/ton 🔴**[VERIFY]** (competitive with incineration/landfilling)
- **Product sales**: Oil, biochar, recovered metals

#### **5. DASRI - Healthcare Waste** (5,000-10,000 tons/year potential)[8]

---

**📋 WHAT IS DASRI?**

**DASRI** = **D**échets d'**A**ctivités de **S**oins à **R**isques **I**nfectieux
**English**: Infectious Healthcare Waste

**Definition**: Medical waste that may contain microorganisms capable of causing infections, requiring specialized treatment before disposal.

**Examples**: Used syringes, IV bags, surgical instruments, blood-soaked materials, PPE from infectious disease treatment, laboratory cultures.

**Regulatory Status**: Highly regulated under French public health code; requires authorized collection, treatment, and disposal.

---

**Val d'Oise Medical Infrastructure**[8]:
- Multiple hospitals and clinics in Val d'Oise department
- **ABARIS**: Specialized DASRI collection company operating in Val d'Oise since 1993
- **Séché Healthcare, Ecodas**: National DASRI processors

**Current DASRI Treatment**:
- Collection → Sterilization/autoclave → Incineration → Disposal
- **100% incineration** (CO₂ intensive, no material recovery)
- **Treatment cost**: €150-250/ton 🔴**[VERIFY]** (true cost €250-400/ton)

**Urban Rig Opportunity for DASRI**:
- **Pre-sterilized DASRI** (after autoclave treatment) could be processed by Urban Rig
- Convert post-treatment medical waste (mostly plastics: IV bags, syringes, PPE) into valuable oil
- **Material recovery** vs. complete destruction
- **Lower CO₂ emissions** vs. incineration

**Challenges**:
- ⚠️ Regulatory approval required (strict DASRI regulations)
- ⚠️ Must demonstrate equivalent or better treatment than incineration
- ⚠️ Potential concerns about residual contamination

_**Estimated DASRI Volume (Val d'Oise region)**: 5,000-10,000 tons/year_

_**BASIS**: France generates ~170,000 tons/year DASRI nationally. Val d'Oise (population ~1.25M) represents ~2% of national total. **Requires validation with ABARIS, Séché Healthcare, or Val d'Oise health authority.**_

**NOTE**: DASRI integration requires additional regulatory work and may be **Phase 2 opportunity** after proving MSW/commercial waste processing.

## 3.3 Equipment Configuration and Technology

### 3.3.1 Selected Configuration: 2 × 100 Tons/Day Units (Phased Deployment)

**Rationale for Dual Configuration**[9]:
- ✅ **Risk mitigation**: First Urban Rig deployment in France requires proof of concept
- ✅ **Operational redundancy**: One unit continues during maintenance (97% capacity utilization vs 93% for single unit)
- ✅ **Feedstock optimization**: Process plastic-rich waste separately from mixed MSW to maximize oil yield
- ✅ **Phased investment**: Spread €28M total CAPEX over 3-4 years (€13-18M Unit 1, €11-15M Unit 2)
- ✅ **Financing accessibility**: Easier to secure €13-18M initial funding vs €21M upfront for single 200T unit
- ✅ **Stakeholder confidence**: Demonstrate success to ADP, TotalEnergies, municipalities before full commitment

**Alternative Considered**: 1 × 200 tons/day unit
- Lower CAPEX (€18-24M total, 25% less)
- Lower OPEX (€34/ton vs €42/ton, 24% less)
- Faster payback (0.9-1.2 years vs 1.2-1.6 years)
- **Rejected for CDG pilot**: Higher upfront risk, no redundancy, France market unproven

**Future Deployments**: Once proven at CDG, subsequent installations (Lyon, Marseille, etc.) can use 1 × 200T configuration to maximize financial efficiency.

_See detailed configuration analysis in **Annex: Equipment Configuration Comparison** [9]._

**Key Technical Specifications**[10]:

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Processing Capacity** | 2 × 100 tons/day continuous |
| **Annual Throughput** | 73,000 tons/year (365-day operation, 5% downtime) |

**Competitive Advantages vs. Batch Pyrolysis**:
- ✅ **Continuous operation**: 24/7 vs. batch cycles (higher throughput per unit)

- ✅ **Energy efficiency**: Syngas continuously recycled vs. batch startup/shutdown losses

**Competitive Advantages vs. Incineration**:
- ✅ **Material recovery**: 95% recovery vs. 10-15% (ash/metals only)
- ✅ **CO₂ reduction**: 75-90% lower emissions (pyrolysis oil displaces fossil fuels)
- ✅ **Air quality**: Minimal NOx, SOx, particulates vs. incineration emissions
- ✅ **No subsidies required**: Economically viable on gate fees + product sales vs. incineration requires energy revenue subsidies

## 3.6 Regulatory and Permitting Strategy

### 3.6.1 ICPE Permitting Requirements

**Classification**: Installations Classées pour la Protection de l'Environnement (ICPE)[13]

**Expected Classification**: Rubrique 2771 (waste treatment, thermal processing)
- **Authorization required**: Préfectoral authorization (not simple declaration)
- **Timeline**: 12-24 months for full ICPE authorization
- **Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA)**: Required for capacity >100 tons/day

**ICPE Dossier Components**:
1. **Technical description** (equipment, process, safety systems)
2. **Environmental impact study** (air quality, water, noise, traffic)
3. **Hazard study** (fire, explosion, chemical risks)
4. **Waste management plan** (inputs accepted, outputs generated, treatment of residues)
5. **Public consultation** (60-day public comment period)

**CDG Site Advantages for Permitting**:
- ✅ Existing industrial zoning (not greenfield)
- ✅ Air quality monitoring infrastructure already in place (airport requirements)
- ✅ Waste management infrastructure precedent
- ✅ Lower public opposition (industrial zone vs. residential)

---

### 3.6.2 Air Quality and Emissions Compliance

**Relevant Regulations**:
- **EU Industrial Emissions Directive (IED)**: Best Available Techniques (BAT) for waste treatment
- **French Air Quality Standards**: Prefectural air quality plan (Paris region)
- **Low Emission Zone (LEZ)**: Paris ZFE-m (Zone à Faibles Émissions mobilité)

**Urban Rig Emissions Control**[10]:
- **Integrated exhaust gas treatment**: Activated carbon filtration, catalytic oxidation
- **Ceramic water filtration**: Closed-loop water recycling, treated discharge
- **Emissions profile**: <10% of incineration levels for NOx, SOx, particulates, dioxins

**Monitoring Requirements**:
- Continuous emissions monitoring system (CEMS)
- Quarterly reporting to DREAL (Regional Environmental Authority)
- Annual third-party emissions audit

**Expected Outcome**: Urban Rig emissions significantly below ICPE limits and competitive with best-in-class incineration.

## 3.8 Risk Mitigation Strategies

### 3.8.1 Technical Risks

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|-------------------|
| **Equipment performance below expectations** | • Urban Rig proven technology (existing deployments)<br>• Performance guarantees in equipment contract<br>• Phased deployment allows validation before Unit 2 |
| **Feedstock quality variability** | • Dual unit configuration allows feedstock optimization<br>• Pre-processing/screening to remove contaminants<br>• Flexible process parameters (600°C can handle wide range) |
| **Emissions non-compliance** | • Conservative design (target <50% of ICPE limits)<br>• Redundant emissions control systems<br>• Continuous monitoring with alarms |

---

### 3.8.2 Market Risks

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|-------------------|
| **Waste supply insufficient** | • Multiple waste streams (airport, MSW, industrial, composites)<br>• Total addressable market (120-200k tons/year) >> design capacity (73k tons/year)<br>• Long-term contracts with ADP, municipalities |
| **Oil price collapse** | • Dual revenue model (tipping fees cover OPEX, oil sales = upside)<br>• Long-term offtake with TotalEnergies (price floor negotiated)<br>• SAF mandate provides price support (regulatory demand) |
| **Regulatory changes** | • Trend is favorable (EU circular economy, SAF mandates, landfill bans)<br>• Loi AGEC (France) increases TGAP tax on landfilling/incineration (benefits Urban Rig)<br>• Early mover advantage before regulations tighten |

---

### 3.8.3 Financial Risks

| Risk | Mitigation Strategy |
|------|-------------------|
| **Cost overruns (CAPEX)** | • Phased deployment limits exposure (€13-18M Phase 1 vs €28M total)<br>• Fixed-price EPC contract for construction<br>• 15-20% contingency in CAPEX budget |
| **Revenue ramp slower than projected** | • Conservative ramp assumptions (50% Year 1, 100% Year 2)<br>• Secured waste contracts before commissioning<br>• TotalEnergies MOU for oil offtake de-risks product revenue |
| **Financing unavailable** | • Multiple financing sources (equity, green bonds, ADEME grants, EIB)<br>• Phased approach allows self-financing of Phase 2 from Phase 1 cash flow<br>• Strong project economics (1.2-1.6 year payback) attractive to lenders |

## 3.10 Strategic Importance and Vision

### 3.10.1 CDG as Flagship European Demonstration

**Why CDG is Strategically Critical**:
- ✅ **First Urban Rig deployment in France** - sets precedent for regulatory approval, stakeholder buy-in
- ✅ **High-visibility location** - CDG is Europe's 2nd busiest airport, international attention
- ✅ **Circular economy showcase** - waste-to-SAF loop with Air France is compelling narrative
- ✅ **Multi-stakeholder validation** - success with ADP, TotalEnergies, Île-de-France validates model for replication
- ✅ **Technology-agnostic waste stream** - diverse waste sources (airport, MSW, industrial, composites) prove versatility

**Flagship Status Benefits**:
- Government support (ADEME grants, ministerial endorsements)
- Media coverage (environmental success story)
- Investor confidence (de-risks subsequent deployments)
- Customer acquisition (municipalities see proven French deployment)

---

### 3.10.2 Replication Pathway (Post-CDG Success)

**Phase 1 (Years 1-5): CDG Proof of Concept**
- 1 site (CDG), 2 units, 200 tons/day

**Phase 2 (Years 5-10): Regional Expansion**
- **5-10 sites** across France (Lyon, Marseille, Lille, Toulouse, Bordeaux)
- **1,000-2,000 tons/day** total capacity
- Target: 5% of French incineration capacity

**Phase 3 (Years 10-15): National Rollout**
- **50-100 sites** nationwide
- **10,000+ tons/day** total capacity
- Become standard solution for residual waste in France

**Phase 4 (Years 15+): European Expansion**
- Leverage French success for Germany, Spain, Italy, UK deployments
- **Urban Rig as leading alternative to incineration across Europe**

---

### 3.10.3 Alignment with Policy and Market Trends

**EU Policies Driving Urban Rig Adoption**:
- ✅ **EU Circular Economy Action Plan**: 65% recycling target by 2035 (requires alternatives to incineration)
- ✅ **EU SAF Mandates**: 2% by 2025, 6% by 2030, 20% by 2035 (creates demand for circular feedstocks)
- ✅ **EU Landfill Directive**: Landfill ban on recyclables by 2030 (wind turbine blades, plastics)
- ✅ **EU Waste Framework Directive**: "Waste hierarchy" prioritizes recovery over disposal

**French Policies Driving Urban Rig Adoption**:
- ✅ **Loi AGEC (2020)**: Circular economy law reducing incineration, increasing reuse/recycling
- ✅ **TGAP Tax Escalation**: €65/ton landfilling, €15/ton incineration (rising annually) - makes Urban Rig gate fees competitive
- ✅ **ADEME Grants**: 20-80% CAPEX support for circular economy pilot projects
- ✅ **Paris Air Quality Plan**: Reducing incineration emissions, LEZ compliance

**Market Trends**:
- ⬆️ **Declining landfill capacity**: France running out of landfill space, driving tipping fees higher
- ⬆️ **Incineration opposition**: Public resistance to new incinerators, air quality concerns
- ⬆️ **SAF demand surge**: Aviation industry under pressure to decarbonize, premium for circular feedstocks
- ⬆️ **Composite waste crisis**: 50-100k tons/year wind turbine blades with no solution by 2030

**CDG Project Timing**: Perfectly positioned to capitalize on these converging policy and market drivers.
