## 12.3 Preliminary Site Layout Concept

**Site Area**: 1-2 hectares

**Functional Zones**:
1. **Waste Receiving Area**:
   - Truck scales
   - Inspection bay
   - Enclosed waste bunker (500 m³ capacity, 3-5 days storage)
   - Negative pressure + odor control
2. **Processing Area**:
   - Urban Rig reactor (enclosed building)
   - Feed conveyors and pre-processing (magnetic separator, optional shredder)
   - Control room
3. **Product Storage**:
   - Oil tanks (2-3 tanks × 50 tons)
   - Carbon silos or big-bag storage
   - Metal storage area
4. **Utilities and Support**:
   - Electrical substation
   - Water treatment and recycling
   - Emissions monitoring and exhaust stack
   - Maintenance workshop
   - Office and welfare facilities
5. **Perimeter**:
   - Access road and parking
   - Security fencing and gate
   - Landscaping and buffer zones
   - Stormwater management

**Layout Considerations**:
- Logical flow: Waste in → Processing → Products out
- Separation of truck traffic and personnel areas
- Buffer zones from property boundary (50-100m depending on zoning)
- Emergency access for fire brigade
- Future expansion capability

## 12.6 Environmental Impact Pre-Assessment

**Summary of Key Findings** (from preliminary environmental study):

**Positive Impacts**:
- 80-90% GHG reduction vs conventional waste treatment
- 70,000 tons/year waste diversion from landfill/incineration
- Elimination of landfill methane emissions
- Air quality improvement (vs incineration)
- No wastewater discharge (closed-loop)
- Job creation (10-13 direct, 20-30 indirect)

**Potential Concerns and Mitigation**:
- **Air emissions**: Integrated exhaust treatment + CEMS ensures compliance
- **Noise**: Enclosed equipment + buffer zones → <55 dB at boundary
- **Odor**: Enclosed waste storage + rapid processing → minimal odor
- **Traffic**: Waste delivery trucks (managed schedule, daytime only)
- **Visual impact**: Industrial design, landscaping, appropriate location

**Biodiversity**:
- Site selection in existing industrial area minimizes habitat impact
- No rare or protected species identified in preliminary survey

## 12.11 Glossary of Terms

**ADEME**: Agence de la Transition Écologique (French Environment and Energy Management Agency)

**BAT**: Best Available Techniques (EU standard for industrial emissions)

**Biochar**: Carbon-rich solid produced by pyrolysis of biomass; can be used as soil amendment or fuel

**Carbon Black**: Solid carbon residue from pyrolysis; similar to soot or coal

**CEMS**: Continuous Emissions Monitoring System

**CO₂e**: Carbon dioxide equivalent (measures total greenhouse gas impact)

**CSE**: Comité Social et Économique (workplace social and economic committee in France)

**DREAL**: Direction Régionale de l'Environnement, de l'Aménagement et du Logement (Regional environmental authority)

**EBITDA**: Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation, and Amortization

**EPC**: Engineering, Procurement, Construction

**EWC**: European Waste Catalogue (classification codes for waste types)

**ICPE**: Installations Classées pour la Protection de l'Environnement (classified installations for environmental protection)

**IRR**: Internal Rate of Return

**Loi AGEC**: Anti-Waste Law for a Circular Economy (French law)

**MSW**: Municipal Solid Waste

**NPV**: Net Present Value

**Pyrolysis**: Thermal decomposition of materials in the absence of oxygen

**SCADA**: Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (industrial control system)

**SNBC**: Stratégie Nationale Bas-Carbone (French National Low-Carbon Strategy)

**Tipping Fee**: Fee charged to waste generators for accepting and processing their waste

**Urban Rig**: Proprietary continuous pyrolysis technology for waste conversion

**Valorea**: Name of the joint venture company (from "valorisation" = value recovery)

**WTE**: Waste-to-Energy (typically refers to incineration with energy recovery)

**WTV**: Waste-to-Value (broader concept including material and energy recovery)
