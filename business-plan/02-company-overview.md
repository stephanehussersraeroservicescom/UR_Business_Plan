# 2. Company Overview

## 2.1 Valorea: A Strategic Joint Venture

Valorea will be established as a **50/50 joint venture** between:
- **Urban Rig Co. Ltd** (Technology partner)
- **French partner entity** (Operational partner)

The company will operate under French commercial law with Paris-based management and a dedicated operational team.

## 2.2 Urban Rig Co. Ltd - Technology Partner

### Mission
Urban Rig is a global innovator in advanced waste valorization technology, dedicated to transforming waste management through continuous steam-cracking processes that recover maximum value from mixed waste streams.

### Technology Contribution
- **Patented continuous pyrolysis system**
- Oxygen-free steam-cracking process operating at 600°C
- Automated feed and extraction systems
- Integrated ceramic filtration and water recycling
- Exhaust gas treatment technology

### Expertise and Support
- Technical installation and commissioning
- Operator training programs
- Ongoing technical support and optimization
- Global knowledge transfer from other installations
- Continuous R&D and system improvements

## 2.3 French Partner Entity - Operational Partner

### Role and Responsibilities
- **Regulatory compliance**: Navigate French and EU environmental regulations
- **Permitting**: Secure ICPE registration and all necessary approvals
- **Local integration**: Build relationships with waste suppliers, offtakers, and authorities
- **Operations management**: Day-to-day facility management and staffing
- **Stakeholder relations**: Engage with local communities, government, and industry

### Strategic Value
- Deep understanding of French waste management ecosystem
- Established relationships with regional waste generators
- Expertise in French environmental law and permitting
- Access to local labor and service providers
- Integration with existing waste infrastructure

## 2.4 Legal Structure

### Corporate Form
- **Legal entity**: Société par Actions Simplifiée (SAS) or similar structure
- **Registered office**: Paris, Île-de-France
- **Governance**: Board of directors with equal representation from both partners

### Ownership and Control
| Partner | Equity Share | Key Responsibilities |
|---------|--------------|---------------------|
| Urban Rig Co. Ltd | 50% | Technology, technical support, global expertise |
| French partner | 50% | Operations, compliance, local integration |

### Decision-Making
- Major strategic decisions require consensus of both partners
- Operational decisions delegated to local management team
- Technical decisions guided by Urban Rig standards and best practices

## 2.5 Management Structure

### Executive Leadership
- **General Manager**: Oversees all operations, reports to board
- **Technical Director**: Urban Rig representative, ensures technology performance
- **Operations Manager**: Manages day-to-day facility operations
- **Commercial Director**: Manages waste intake and byproduct sales

### Operational Team
Estimated team size: **6-8 full-time employees**
- Shift operators (3-4 positions)
- Maintenance technician (1-2 positions)
- Logistics coordinator (1 position)
- HSE (Health, Safety, Environment) manager (1 position)

## 2.6 Operational Model

### Integration with French Waste Management System

**Waste Sourcing**:
- Municipal contracts with Île-de-France waste authorities
- Commercial agreements with private waste collectors
- Direct intake from CDG Airport operations
- Industrial waste partnerships

**Byproduct Offtake**:
- Sustainable oil: Sold to refiners, chemical producers, or energy companies
- Carbon black: Industrial buyers (cement, steel, activated carbon production)
- Recovered metals: Scrap metal dealers and recyclers
- Potential for carbon credit generation

**Value Proposition**:
- Tipping fee revenue from waste acceptance
- Product sales revenue
- Emissions reduction credits (if applicable)
- Reduced waste management costs for the region

## 2.7 Strategic Partnerships

### Potential Partnership Categories

**Waste Supply Partners**:
- Île-de-France waste management authorities
- Private waste collection companies
- CDG Airport and affiliated businesses
- Industrial and commercial waste generators

**Technology and Service Partners**:
- Equipment maintenance providers
- Laboratory services for quality control
- Logistics and transportation companies
- Energy utilities

**Offtake Partners**:
- Refineries and fuel distributors
- Industrial buyers (carbon black, metals)
- Carbon credit brokers and registries

## 2.8 Valorea's Mission and Values

### Mission Statement
*"To demonstrate that waste is not an end, but a beginning - transforming what others discard into valuable resources that power the circular economy and protect our environment."*

### Core Values
- **Innovation**: Leveraging cutting-edge technology to solve persistent problems
- **Sustainability**: Prioritizing environmental benefit in every decision
- **Transparency**: Open communication with stakeholders and communities
- **Excellence**: Commitment to operational excellence and continuous improvement
- **Partnership**: Collaborative approach with all stakeholders

### Strategic Vision
Valorea aims to become the reference model for urban waste valorization in France, demonstrating that:
- Mixed waste can be profitably processed without incineration or landfilling
- Circular economy principles can be economically viable
- Urban areas can host clean, safe, advanced waste processing
- France can lead Europe in waste innovation
